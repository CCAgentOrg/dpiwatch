# DPI Brief — October 29, 2026

**Coverage window:** 28 October 2026, 08:00 – 29 October 2026, 08:00 IST.

Five developments crossed the watchlist across trust, sectoral infrastructure, commerce, payments and governance — led by the news industry's constitutional challenge to the DPDP Act and a planned AgriStack-based overhaul of fertiliser distribution.

## 1. News publishers' DPDP challenge reaches the Supreme Court; Vaishnaw defends the law (L7 — trust)

The Digital News Publishers Association, representing 21 news organisations, moved the Supreme Court on 27 October challenging the DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, and the Court has sought the government's response. [The petition targets](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/dnpa-challenges-dpdp-act-in-supreme-court-over-press-freedom-risks-ws-l-113493.htm) the absence of a journalism exemption, Section 36 read with Rule 23 (which publishers say could expose confidential sources when the government seeks information), and Section 44(3), which rewrote the RTI Act's Section 8(1)(j) from a public-interest test into a blanket "personal information" exemption. [An NDTV explainer](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/explainer-why-news-publishers-challenged-data-protection-law-in-supreme-court-12242810) lays out the seven asks. On 28 October, IT Minister [Ashwini Vaishnaw said the Act does not infringe press freedom](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/digital-personal-data-protection-act-does-not-infringe-on-press-freedom-ashwini-vaishnaw/article71637035.ece), that the Constitution guarantees journalistic independence, and that the Centre is open to changes.

Consumer stakes: Section 44(3)'s RTI rewrite narrows what any citizen can obtain about DPI systems. Note also that the Act's security safeguards and penalty provisions come into force 18 months after the 13 November 2025 commencement notification — [still not in force as of 28 October](http://legal.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/law-policy/dpdp-framework-needs-clearer-cyber-security-benchmarks-say-legal-experts/135260160), putting the compliance clock near May 2027.

## 2. PM to push states on AgriStack-based fertiliser entitlements (L5 — sectoral)

[The Financial Express reports](https://www.financialexpress.com/policy/economy-pm-to-push-states-on-fertiliser-subsidy-reforms-targets-agri-stack-rollout-4371585) that the Prime Minister will advise states to adopt a "Framework for Fertilizer Sale" (FFS) built on AgriStack: farmers book fertiliser on a mobile app using landholding and cropping data, receive a QR-coded quota token, and collect it at a retailer's PoS with Aadhaar biometric authentication, each quota tied to a designated distributor. FFS pilots ran in 222 districts across 29 states and UTs in Kharif 2026, with fertiliser sales down 28%; Madhya Pradesh, which fully implemented the framework, cut consumption from 3.6 to 3.2 million tonnes and saved ₹764 crore. The iFMS backbone covers over 14 crore Aadhaar-linked buyers and 2.5 lakh retailers. With the FY26 subsidy bill at ₹2.17 lakh crore and 77% of FY27's budgeted ₹1.71 lakh crore already spent, the item tops the agenda at the chief secretaries' conference in the last week of December.

The consumer test: this ends effectively unrestricted purchase of subsidised fertiliser. The risk is exclusion — farmers whose land records are wrong or missing in AgriStack get no quota, so land-record correction channels become critical infrastructure, not paperwork.

## 3. GeM cuts transaction charges, widens MSE exemptions (L4 — commerce)

Per a Ministry of Commerce press release issued at [GeM Samvad 2026 with Laghu Udyog Bharati](https://ddindia.co.in/2026/10/gem-cuts-transaction-charges-to-0-30-raises-exemption-threshold-to-%E2%82%B910-lakh-for-mses), the Government e-Marketplace has cut transaction charges from 0.50% to 0.30% of order value, raised the charge-exemption threshold from ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh, and capped charges for orders above ₹10 crore at ₹3 lakh — down from an earlier maximum of ₹72.50 lakh. Nearly 97% of orders already attract no transaction charges. GeM has also integrated with all five TReDS exchanges (per the Union Budget 2026–27 announcement), alongside GeM SAHAY's collateral-free lending against purchase orders — the working-capital side matters as much as the fee cut for small suppliers.

## 4. Zimbabwe in advanced talks to build its national payments system on UPI (L2 — payments)

Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe Governor John Mushayavanhu [told The Herald the central bank is in discussions with NIPL](https://www.thezimbabwean.co/2026/10/rbz-plans-single-qr-code-for-all-payments-under-india-backed-platform), NPCI International's arm, to deploy UPI technology as the foundation of Zimbabwe's National Payments Digital Public Infrastructure — one QR code across participating banks and mobile money wallets, with 31 October set as the deadline to conclude a deal. No agreement exists yet; watch whether the deadline holds.

## 5. Parliamentary panel to review RTI Act implementation and CIC functioning (L6 — governance)

Per a [Lok Sabha bulletin issued on 28 October](https://government.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/governance/parliamentary-panel-to-review-rti-act-implementation-cic-functioning-amid-case-pendency/135271055), the Parliamentary Standing Committee on Personnel, Public Grievances, Law and Justice will examine RTI Act implementation and the Central Information Commission's functioning — over 10,000 cases are pending before the CIC, which is now at its full sanctioned strength of 11 information commissioners. The committee will also review grievance redressal, including strengthening awareness of CPGRAMS and CPENGRAMS, appraise the e-Courts Mission Mode Project, and examine AI use in justice delivery. The juxtaposition with item 1 is hard to miss: the same RTI architecture the DPDP petition challenges is now under parliamentary review.

## Seven-layer scan

- **L1 Identity:** A UIDAI official [told The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/star-singer-with-us-citizenship-visiting-india-since-2022-without-filing-form-c/articleshow/135245186.cms) that Aadhaar establishes identity, not citizenship — foreign nationals may enrol after 182 days of residence with validity linked to their visa, and there is no surrender provision after a citizenship change. For JEE Main 2027 registration, NTA is directing candidates to [complete Re-KYC via DigiLocker/JanParichay/e-Pramaan (MeriPehchaan)](https://www.shiksha.com/engineering/articles/aadhaar-re-kyc-via-digilocker-for-jee-main-2027-registration-blogId-245427).
- **L2 Payments:** NPCI signed an [MoU with IIIT Hyderabad on 28 October](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/iiit-hyderabad-and-npci-sign-mou-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-in-digital-payments) to strengthen payments cybersecurity through the institute's Cyber Manthan Center — joint R&D, digital forensics research and workforce training. Cross-border acceptance [is also reported coming to Uzbekistan](https://www.uzdaily.uz/en/indian-tourists-to-pay-in-uzbekistan-via-upi) via UZQR.
- **L3 Documents:** The Ministry of Power [released NEDSF 2026](https://solarquarter.com/2026/10/28/ministry-of-power-releases-nedsf-2026-to-standardize-electricity-data-sharing-across-india), a framework standardising electricity data sharing through APIs and secure platforms such as API Setu and NAPIX, with formal agreements and in-India processing required for Tier 2 data.
- **L5 Sectoral:** The fertiliser story above; no new ABHA or ABDM release located in the window.
- **L7 Trust:** Legal experts [called for clearer cybersecurity benchmarks](http://legal.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/law-policy/dpdp-framework-needs-clearer-cyber-security-benchmarks-say-legal-experts/135260160) within the DPDP framework, flagging gaps in negligence assessment, security standards and breach-reporting coordination ahead of enforcement.

**Watch next:** the Supreme Court's handling of the DNPA plea and any amendment signals; the FFS framework's formal adoption at the December chief secretaries' conference; whether a Zimbabwe–NIPL deal lands by 31 October; the DPDP rules pipeline as the ~May 2027 enforcement date approaches; and WCD Special Campaign 6 grievance-closure totals after tomorrow's 30 October deadline.
