# DPI Brief — November 04, 2026

**Top DPI headlines for November 4, 2026** — Nagaland formally accepts Aadhaar App credentials as government ID, Panchayati Raj benefits pivot to voter IDs and biometrics, lender Fibe joins the UPI app race with credit rails, CDSL takes a strategic stake in ONDC, and the DPDP fight over journalism reaches the Supreme Court and the editors' table.

## [L1 · Identity] Nagaland makes Aadhaar App credentials valid ID across government

Nagaland's Home Department has directed all departments, district administrations and implementing agencies to accept Aadhaar verifiable credentials shared or verified through the new Aadhaar App as valid identity proof ([Eastern Mirror Nagaland](https://www.easternmirrornagaland.com/aadhaar-app-now-valid-id-proof-in-nagaland), [The Morung Express](https://www.morungexpress.com/aadhaar-app-credentials-valid-id-proof-nagaland-government), [Nokinketer](https://nokinketer.in/2026/11/03/nagaland-directs-departments-to-accept-aadhaar-app-as-valid-identity-proof)). It invokes UIDAI circular HQ-30011/3/2025-AU-HO (April 30, 2026), grounding validity in the IT Act, 2000 and the Aadhaar Act, 2016. The operational novelty is selective disclosure: holders share only the fields a verifier needs via a cryptographically signed credential, without transferring the 12-digit Aadhaar number to the offline verification entity. Since UIDAI retired mAadhaar on July 1, this is the first state-level compliance order for the new app — exactly the verifier-side adoption the credential architecture needs, because a QR code is only as useful as the officer willing to scan it. The replication test: other states' home departments, and acceptance beyond government counters into banks and employers.

## [L1/L6 · Identity meets governance] Panchayati Raj benefits to ride on voter IDs and biometrics, not Aadhaar

Union Panchayati Raj Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh said beneficiary identification for panchayati raj schemes will use voter ID cards (EPIC) with biometric authentication rather than Aadhaar, to curb fraud ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/welfare-payments-will-be-based-on-voter-ids-not-aadhaar-says-panchayati-raj-minister/article71662258.ece), [Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/biometrics-voter-ids-for-beneficiary-payments-not-aadhaar-minister-rajiv-ranjan-singh-10949512)). The remarks came at the National Workshop on People's Plan Campaign 2026-27; the ministry notes no Panchayati Raj scheme transfers money directly to individual accounts, though schemes under the minister's other portfolio — Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying — still mandate Aadhaar for payments. Read alongside the November 1 enforcement of biometric Aadhaar authentication for subsidised LPG refills, India is now running two parallel identity gates for welfare: Aadhaar where DBT flows, EPIC-plus-biometrics where panchayat-level benefits do. The consumer stakes are duplication and exclusion — two registries, two update trails, and an EPIC-Aadhaar seeding history that has itself been contentious. If the intent is fraud control, the measurable question is whether exclusion falls — or simply changes address.

## [L2 · Payments] Fibe enters the UPI app market with credit rails built in

Pune-based digital lender Fibe has launched UPI payments after NPCI approved it as a Third-Party Application Provider ([Indian Startup News](https://indianstartupnews.com/news/fibe-gets-npci-approval-launches-upi-payments-via-bank-accounts-rupay-credit-cards-and-credit-lines-12776312)). Users can pay from bank accounts, RuPay credit cards and eligible pre-sanctioned credit lines linked to UPI — riding RBI's 2023 framework enabling bank credit lines on UPI. It lands in a market at record scale but extreme concentration: UPI processed 25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore in October, crossing ₹1 lakh crore in average daily value for the first time, while PhonePe, Google Pay and Paytm together held roughly 86% share in September. With NPCI's 30% per-app market cap due December 31, 2026, and the 0.4% MDR on large merchants deferred, the TPAP gate stays open precisely when the pecking order is supposed to loosen. For users, a lending app inside a payments app is convenience with a price tag: credit-line UPI spends are loans — interest set by the bank, missed repayments on the credit report.

## [L4 · Commerce] CDSL takes a strategic stake in ONDC

CDSL — the depository behind 19.34 crore demat accounts, which added about 75 lakh new accounts in the September quarter — disclosed a strategic investment in the Open Network for Digital Commerce ([CNBC TV18 via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/cnbctv:9689ae74d094b:0)). A market-infrastructure institution buying into the commerce layer matters twice over: it extends the pattern of established DPI operators bankrolling newer network layers, and it hands ONDC a strategic investor whose core business is verified ownership records — thematically adjacent to DigiDukaan's push to formalise kirana procurement. Context from the same week: DPIIT and ONDC pressed 40+ FMCG majors at an October 30 roundtable to scale DigiDukaan, which connects over 21,000 retailers to 450+ brands across Hyderabad, Jaipur and Mumbai, with Hyderabad alone crossing 16,000 retailers at 73–78% monthly repeat-order rates ([Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/news/dpiit-ondc-seek-fmcg-industry-push-to-digitise-procurement-for-14-million-kiranas/4373957)). The number to watch is purpose: whether CDSL's stake buys network participation, settlement infrastructure, or plain balance-sheet support.

## [L7 · Trust] DPDP and journalism: Supreme Court notices, an NBDA plea, and a ministerial olive branch

The confrontation over the DPDP Act's impact on reporting escalated on three fronts this week. The Supreme Court issued notices to the Law Ministry and MeitY on the Digital News Publishers Association's petition challenging the Act and the DPDP Rules, 2025. The News Broadcasters & Digital Association separately wrote to the Centre seeking an exemption for journalistic activities, warning that consent requirements and data-access powers could hamper reporting and expose confidential sources ([India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/amp/india/story/dpdp-act-exemption-journalists-nbda-news-gathering-press-freedom-3030870-2026-11-02)). And the government invited editors for consultations, assuring no curbs on journalistic freedom ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/govt-invites-editors-for-talks-on-digital-data-privacy-law-assures-no-curbs-on-journalistic-freedom)). The clock matters: consent-manager registration with the Data Protection Board opens November 13, 2026, and the Act's main obligations take effect May 13, 2027. Journalism is the sharpest test case for the consent framework — if investigative reporting needs consent to cite documents, the public's right to know becomes a compliance question. Watch whether the consultations produce a "legitimate uses" notification or an amendment.
