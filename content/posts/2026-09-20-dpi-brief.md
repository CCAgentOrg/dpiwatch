# DPI Brief — September 20, 2026

The last 24 hours saw action across five of India's seven DPI layers, with the payments layer dominating: the government mounted a full-throated defence of the new UPI merchant charges, TRAI finalised its anti-spam rulebook, Aadhaar authentication became mandatory for LPG subsidies, Karnataka committed to a statewide ABDM health-records rollout, and DARPG showcased digital governance learnings from Jammu & Kashmir.

## L2 Payments: Finance Ministry defends UPI MDR, denies US pressure

The Ministry of Finance rejected allegations that American pressure influenced the decision to introduce a 0.4% merchant discount rate (MDR) on select UPI transactions, stating that the [new NPCI guidelines give international credit cards no advantage over RuPay](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/sectors/upi-mdr-finance-ministry-denies-us-pressure-claims-says-foreign-credit-cards-get-no-advantage-over-rupay). Under the framework effective October 15, P2M UPI payments above ₹2,000 attract 0.4% MDR (capped at ₹300 for transactions above ₹75,000); railways, telecom, fuel and insurance see a flat ₹5 charge; capital-market transactions get a concessional 0.02%; and small merchants collecting up to ₹1 lakh monthly via UPI QR are exempt. [SCC Online's explainer](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/09/19/upi-mdr-rules-explained-upi-remains-free-for-users-merchant-payments-above-rs-2000) confirms users themselves pay nothing.

Crucially, the legal architecture is now layered: as [The Times of India notes](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/toi-blogs/policy-prices/digital-payments-a-subsidised-pipeline/articleshow/134352458.cms), the September 14 gazette notification S.O. 5067(E) from the Department of Financial Services makes zero-MDR a statutory right for RuPay debit cards (no limit) and UPI transactions up to ₹2,000 — while everything above sits in a price list that is regulation, not law. The political backlash is building too: the [BJD has protested the changes](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/bjd-protests-upi-changes-says-centre-should-fund-digital-payments/articleshow/134351954.cms), arguing the Centre should fund digital payments directly. The stakes are visible in the scale: UPI processed [24.51 billion transactions worth ₹29.82 lakh crore in August 2026](https://startupfortune.com/upi-will-charge-merchants-a-04-fee-on-big-payments-from-october-15) — a monthly record, up 22% year-on-year across 750+ banks and 554.9 million registered users.

## L1 Identity: Aadhaar goes biometric-mandatory for LPG, eyes global expansion

From October 1, [Aadhaar biometric authentication will be required to receive the subsidy on domestic LPG refills](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/govt-mandates-aadhaar-biometric-authentication-for-lpg-subsidy-from-oct-1-126091900881_1.html), ending the OTP-based era for cooking-gas welfare delivery. The move tightens subsidy targeting but raises the usual questions about authentication failures at the last mile — rural consumers without working fingerprints or connectivity will need reliable fallbacks.

Meanwhile, [UIDAI is working on taking Aadhaar global](https://www.livemint.com/news/india/uidai-plans-to-take-aadhaar-global-what-are-current-rules-for-nris-and-oci-cardholders-11789804614313.html), especially for NRIs and OCI cardholders. UIDAI CEO Saurabh Vijay, speaking at Global Fintech Fest 2026, said daily authentications have jumped from about 7 crore to 10 crore, and the authority is preparing infrastructure to handle 25-30 crore authentications a day — a 3x capacity headroom bet on identity becoming India's most exported DPI.

## L5 Sectoral: Karnataka commits to statewide ABDM health records

Karnataka will [roll out a statewide digital health records program from early next year](https://bangaloremirror.indiatimes.com/bangalore/others/state-plans-digital-health-records-rollout-from-early-next-year/articleshow/134318073.cms), integrating the ABDM-aligned Hospital Management System with the Community Health System. Patients' ABHA numbers will serve as their digital health identity, letting records travel across facilities with consent-based access controls. Trials are underway at district hospitals and community health centres in Vijayapura, Davangere and Mysuru, with a six-week evaluation window before expansion. If it holds, this is the template for how a large state unifies hospital and primary-care records on a single consent framework.

## L7 Trust: TRAI finalises AI-powered anti-spam regulations

TRAI finalised the [Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-trai-truecaller-140-1600-calls-spam-rules) on September 18, its most significant rewrite of the spam rulebook since 2018. Key provisions: call-management apps like Truecaller are barred from blanket-blocking 140- and 1600-series numbers; receiving telecom operators must use AI/ML to flag suspected spam and share the data with the sending operator within two hours under new Regulation 21A; and legacy consumer consent is now recognised. The rules take effect in 30- and 60-day phases after notification, and Truecaller has already [criticised the data-sharing mandate as anti-competitive](https://nokiamob.net/2026/09/19/indias-telecom-regulator-targets-ai-robocalls-and-caller-id-apps) — jurisdictional enforcement against app makers remains the open question.

## L6 Governance: DARPG workshop puts J&K's digital delivery on the map

At a [DARPG workshop in New Delhi, Jammu & Kashmir showcased its digital governance journey](https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/jk-showcases-digital-governance-journey-at-darpg-workshop/) and shared a roadmap for scaling technology-enabled service delivery — a signal that the post-2019 administrative overhaul of J&K's e-governance stack (integrated service portals, digital service delivery at the last mile) is being packaged as a replicable model for other states.

## The throughline

This week's closing pattern is one of institutional hardening: UPI's free-for-consumers promise is now anchored in a gazette notification even as merchant fees arrive; Aadhaar's subsidy role shifts from OTP to biometric; ABDM moves from pilot to statewide commitment; and TRAI converts its anti-spam consultation into enforceable regulation. The DPI stack is maturing from buildout to enforcement — where the next contest will be over who bears the costs.
