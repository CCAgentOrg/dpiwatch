# DPI Brief — July 27, 2026

## Nilekani Task Force: Aadhaar Architect Returns to Fix India's Exam System

**Layer: L6 (Governance) + L1 (Identity)**

Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced a high-powered task force on examination reforms, appointing Nandan Nilekani — the founding chairman of UIDAI and architect of Aadhaar — as its head. The move comes after days of nationwide "Cockroach Janata Party" protests over the NEET 2026 paper leak, which forced the cancellation of the medical entrance exam for 2 million students and was linked to over a dozen student suicides.

The multidisciplinary task force includes experts from technology, space, education, security, and public administration. According to [Swarajya Magazine](https://swarajyamag.com/news-brief/pm-modi-appoints-nandan-nilekani-to-lead-high-powered-task-force-for-nta-exam-reforms-following-neet-controversy), the government will also table the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026 in Parliament tomorrow (27 July) as part of comprehensive measures to prevent future examination irregularities.

The DPI connection is direct: Nilekani's track record — from Aadhaar (L1 Identity) to UPI (L2 Payments) and the GST technology architecture — signals that the government expects technology-driven structural reform of the National Testing Agency (NTA). If this task force mirrors the approach used to build India's DPI stack, expect digital identity verification, encrypted paper delivery chains, and AI-assisted anomaly detection in examination logistics. The appointment also effectively ended the week-long youth protests, with Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan resigning.

## RuPay-on-UPI Credit Cards: Usage Up, Monetization Elusive

**Layer: L2 (Payments)**

A detailed [Mint analysis](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/rupay-credit-cards-upi-monetization-banks-fintechs-credit-card-usage/amp-11784887621642.html) examines the growing tension in India's payments ecosystem: RuPay credit cards linked to UPI are driving higher card usage volumes, but banks and fintechs are struggling to build a sustainable revenue model from the low-value transaction base that UPI thrives on.

The report highlights that while RuPay-on-UPI has successfully brought millions of new users into the formal credit system — a core DPI objective — the economics remain challenging. UPI's zero-MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) policy for small merchants, combined with the inherently low ticket sizes typical of UPI transactions, compress margins for card issuers. The piece argues that the monetization question for RuPay credit cards on UPI is becoming the next frontier of debate between NPCI, RBI, and the fintech ecosystem.

Separately, a [Livemint feature](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/travelling-abroad-france-mauritius-qatar-uae-greece-bhutan-full-list-countries-upi-adopt-benefit-indian-tourist-students-11784649675553.html) catalogued the expanding list of countries where UPI is now accepted for Indian travellers, underscoring NPCI International Payments' (NIPL) progress in linking UPI with foreign payment systems for both P2P remittances and P2M transactions.

## Census 2027 Goes Digital: Self-Enumeration Ties Into India's Identity Stack

**Layer: L1 (Identity) + L3 (Documents)**

[The Hindu reported](https://www.thehindu.com/videos/watch-census-2027-how-tamil-nadu-residents-can-complete-self-enumeration-online/article71269409.ece) that India's Census 2027 is going fully digital for the first time, with Tamil Nadu residents now able to complete the self-enumeration process entirely online before officials visit. The process involves mobile number verification (OTP-based, likely leveraging the telecom-DPI linkage), geolocation marking on a map, and the issuance of a Self-Enumeration ID.

This is significant for the DPI ecosystem: while the Census is not itself a DPI layer, the digital self-enumeration workflow relies on mobile verification infrastructure (telecom-DPI linkage) and generates a digital identifier that could interoperate with existing identity and document layers. The Census 2027 digital approach, if scaled nationally, could become one of the largest data collection exercises built on top of India's digital infrastructure.

## Agriculture Ministry Roundtable Pushes for Faster GM/GE Crop Approvals

**Layer: L5 (Sectoral — AgriStack)**

A high-level roundtable organised by the Trust for Advancement of Agricultural Sciences (TAAS), in partnership with ICAR and the Federation of Seed Industry of India (FSII), called for science-based regulatory reforms and accelerated approval processes for genetically modified and genome-edited crops. As reported by [The News Mill](https://thenewsmill.com/2026/07/agriculture-ministry-roundtable-calls-for-faster-approvals-for-gm-and-genome-edited-crops/), participants agreed that India's biotechnology regulatory system needs to be more efficient, transparent, and globally competitive.

The roundtable's recommendations will be compiled into a policy document for submission to regulatory authorities. This is relevant to the AgriStack DPI layer (L5) because the eventual digitisation of agricultural data, farmer registries, and crop science databases depends on having a clear regulatory environment for the very technologies — GM and genome-edited crops — that generate much of the data AgriStack is designed to manage.

## Sitharaman Says Budget Has Buffers; No Revision Needed

**Layer: L2 (Payments — Fiscal)**

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman stated that there is no immediate need to revise Union Budget numbers despite risks from erratic monsoons and elevated global oil prices, as the government has set aside sufficient fiscal buffers for shocks, [reports the Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/news/economy/policy/budget-has-buffers-to-tackle-global-risks-no-need-to-revisit-its-budget-estimates-yet-says-sitharaman/articleshow/132644018.cms). She reinforced confidence in India's fiscal position — a relevant signal for the DPI ecosystem, where central government funding continues to underwrite infrastructure development across all seven layers.
