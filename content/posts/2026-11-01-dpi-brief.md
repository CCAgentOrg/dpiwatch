# DPI Brief — November 01, 2026

**Top DPI headlines for November 1, 2026** — India's biggest-ever pension life-certificate drive switches on this morning, eMudhra's weak-key scandal gets its independent reviewer, UPI's economics becomes dinner-table conversation even as the network books foreign landmarks, and GeM makes selling to government cheaper for small sellers.

## [L1 · Identity] DLC Campaign 5.0 opens today: 2.30 crore life certificates, one face scan at a time

The Department of Pension & Pensioners' Welfare's fifth Nationwide Digital Life Certificate campaign runs November 1–30 across 2,000+ cities and towns — its largest yet, targeting 2.30 crore certificates including 1.50 crore via Aadhaar face authentication ([DoPPW](https://x.com/DOPPW_India/status/2116134345656414673), [NDTV](https://ndtv.in/business-news/digital-life-certificate-campaign-2026-nationwide-drive-begins-november-1-senior-citizens-pensioners-can-submit-jeevan-pramaan-online-from-home-dlc-su-12261757)). Pensioners submit Jeevan Pramaan from home using the Aadhaar Face RD app on Android or iOS, with MeitY and UIDAI providing technical support; "Utsav"-mode camps bundle health check-ups and Aadhaar updates, and doorstep service covers super-senior, sick and divyang pensioners ([campaign guide](https://pressofasia.com/digital-life-certificate-campaign-5-november-2026-pensioners)). The trajectory is real: Campaign 3.0 in 2024 logged 1.62 crore DLCs, 52.73 lakh of them face-authenticated ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2186239)), and last year's 4.0 edition reached roughly 1.9 crore by PIB's later count. With UIDAI's CEO pitching face authentication on Sansad TV [the day before launch](https://x.com/UIDAI/status/2116470257200316755), the message is unambiguous: the annual pensioner pilgrimage to the bank branch is being retired, and the annual proof-of-life now rides on the same face biometric that unlocks the rest of the Aadhaar stack.

## [L7 · Trust] eMudhra names KPMG as independent reviewer; MeitY team and CERT-In advisory frame the official response

eMudhra has appointed KPMG as its independent external reviewer for the weak-key incident that led browsers to distrust a set of its certificates ([PTI](https://money.rediff.com/news/market/emudhra-appoints-kpmg-for-security-key-incident-review/57222320261030), [Fortune India](https://www.fortuneindia.com/business-news/emudhra-appoints-kpmg-to-conduct-independent-review-of-security-keys-incident/167875)). The company committed to an external examination on October 21 and delivered the appointment by regulatory filing on October 30. The technical record now on file: eight intermediate TLS and code-signing certificates carried P-384 private keys generated with insufficient cryptographic randomness — a script error at an April 30, 2025 key-generation ceremony produced keys recoverable almost instantly from their public counterparts ([CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/emudhra-appoints-kpmg-independent-review-weak-key-generation-security-lapse-cyber-attack-security-20023163.htm)). eMudhra says it revoked the affected intermediates within 14 hours and issued no end-entity certificates through them; MeitY has constituted a special team to investigate, while CERT-In maintains that CCA-regulated digital signature certificate issuance and eSign services — the plumbing under India's document layer — were not impacted. The KPMG findings will be the first test of whether "no customer harm" survives an outside look.

## [L2 · Payments] UPI's global march continues while its bill arrives

Two threads on the payments layer this week. Abroad, the Finance Ministry confirmed UPI can now be used for eligible online bookings at the Burj Khalifa and Singapore's Resorts World Sentosa — an acceptance footprint spanning ten countries, with Uzbekistan set to become the eleventh ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/upi-payments-enabled-to-book-tickets-online-for-burj-khalifa-singapore-attractions-finmin/article71648190.ece)). At home, the economics debate went mainstream: NITI Aayog Vice Chairman Ashok Lahiri chief-guested the launch of *Cashless Nation — How UPI Changed Everything* on October 31, citing [September's 24.07 billion UPI transactions worth ₹29.37 lakh crore](https://x.com/NITIAayog/status/2116554055665516631), while Nikhil Kamath's podcast sat down with NPCI's Sohini Rajola alongside PhonePe's Sameer Nigam and Razorpay's Harshil Mathur to ask [who really pays for UPI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/fintech/nikhil-kamath-sits-down-with-the-people-behind-your-payments-network-to-ask-who-really-pays-for-upi-and-what-comes-next/135392690). The question is live: the 0.4% MDR on merchant transactions above ₹2,000 — deferred just before its mid-October start — is now slated to kick in after Diwali, making November the month UPI's zero-cost era formally ends for high-value merchant payments.

## [L4 · Commerce] GeM cuts the cost of selling to government — a catch-up worth having

Saturday's brief covered ONDC's kirana push, but one October commerce reform slipped past: at GeM Samvad 2026 (October 28, with Laghu Udyog Bharati), the Government e-Marketplace [cut transaction charges](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2329780) from 0.50% to 0.30% of order value, raised the exemption threshold from ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh, and capped charges at ₹3 lakh on orders above ₹10 crore — down from a maximum that could touch ₹72.5 lakh ([DD News](https://ddindia.co.in/2026/10/gem-cuts-transaction-charges-to-0-30-raises-exemption-threshold-to-%E2%82%B910-lakh-for-mses)). OEM vendor-assessment fees are down up to 92%, and nearly 97% of orders already attract no transaction charge. For MSE sellers, procurement-platform economics just moved in their direction; the test is whether order flow follows.
