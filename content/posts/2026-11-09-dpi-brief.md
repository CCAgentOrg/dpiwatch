# DPI Brief — November 09, 2026

**Top DPI headlines for November 9, 2026** — the post-Diwali window opens for UPI merchant charges with the notification still pending, NPCI stands up a fourth subsidiary to chase AI and blockchain, Dubai Duty Free pays Indians to pay with UPI, CERT-In quietly gets frontier-AI access, and the identity layer logs a children's biometrics milestone.

## [L2 × L7 · Payments meets Trust] UPI MDR: the day after Diwali arrives, the date hasn't

Diwali was yesterday, and with it the protective ambiguity around UPI merchant charges formally expires. NPCI's October 13 deferral committed to implementing MDR on select person-to-merchant UPI transactions "immediately after Diwali, on a date to be notified separately" — as of this morning, no notification has been issued ([Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/money/upi-mdr-after-diwali-when-will-charges-start-who-pays-and-what-changes-4378408)). The shape of what is coming is settled: up to 0.4% on P2M payments above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300 for transactions of ₹75,000 and above, while payments up to ₹2,000, person-to-person transfers and small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh monthly through UPI QR codes remain exempt ([The Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/inquiry-insights-upi-merchant-fees-and-the-case-for-npci-pricing-governance)). NPCI spent the week defending the levy in the Supreme Court against advocate Anjan Datta's challenge, telling the court that merchants cannot pass the cost to customers and that the charge affects "only a small fraction of users" ([ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/mdr-affects-only-a-small-fraction-of-users-npci-defends-upi-fee-in-supreme-court-enn26110507428), [The Live Nagpur](https://thelivenagpur.com/2026/11/06/merchants-cannot-pass-upi-transaction-costs-to-customers-npci-informs-sc)); the Court had declined a stay in September. The statutory basis — the August 2026 amendment to Section 10A of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, with the September 14 notification retaining free RuPay debit and sub-₹2,000 UPI — means the consumer's protection now depends entirely on which notifications follow. The watch item is disclosure at the till: if implementation lands without a notified date and standardised merchant messaging, small merchants and their customers will discover the charge mid-transaction.

## [L2 · Payments] NPCI's fourth subsidiary: NTSL, the AI-and-blockchain arm

NPCI has formed NPCI Tech Solutions Limited (NTSL), its fourth wholly owned subsidiary after NIPL, NBBL and NBSL, approved by the NPCI board and the Reserve Bank of India, and appointed group CTO Vishal Kanvaty as its MD & CEO ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/npci-launches-tech-arm-for-ai-blockchain-names-vishal-kanvaty-as-ceo-126110401642_1.html)). The mandate announced November 4 and expanded in subsequent reporting: AI, blockchain, digital financial infrastructure, and global capability centre (GCC) services — capabilities beyond NPCI's core retail-payments remit ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/amp/articles/can-npci-replicate-the-upi-success-in-ai-and-blockchain/135664044)). NPCI has been careful not to promise products: no blockchain payment platform has been announced, and UPI users will see no immediate change ([The 420](https://the420.in/npci-launches-new-ai-and-blockchain-subsidiary-appoints-vishal-kanvaty-ceo)). The governance question is worth holding onto: a not-for-profit umbrella body now owns a commercial technology subsidiary selling AI and infrastructure services — to whom, at what prices, and with what firewalls from the pricing power NTSL's parent exercises over UPI, is unasked.

## [L2 · Payments] Dubai Duty Free pays Indians to pay with UPI

NPCI International (NIPL) announced a 10% discount, capped at AED 100, for Indian travellers shopping at Dubai Duty Free when paying through UPI, with Network International as the acquiring partner enabling UPI acceptance at Dubai International and Al Maktoum terminals ([Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/nri/invest/indian-travellers-in-dubai-can-get-up-to-aed-100-off-at-dubai-duty-free-using-upi/articleshow/135563433.cms)). Travellers must activate UPI International or UPI Global on an eligible bank account in their UPI app first. It is a marketing subsidy, but the plumbing underneath is the real story: direct bank-account settlement at foreign point-of-sale terminals, bypassing card networks and forex markups entirely. Cross-border acceptance, not remittance corridors, is where UPI's international push is now concentrated — this follows NIPL's recent extension of UPI to online bookings at UAE and Singapore attractions ([Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/nri/latest-updates/upi-payments-expanded-to-online-bookings-at-tourist-attractions-in-uae-singapore/articleshow/135369519.cms)).

## [L7 × L1 · Trust meets Identity] CERT-In gets frontier AI; TRAI's telecom AI rulebook opens for comments

CERT-In has been given access to Anthropic's Mythos model for cybersecurity work — vulnerability identification and threat response — with the agency stating it will assess use "in a measured manner" given misuse concerns around advanced AI models; access is limited to trusted organisations ([ETCISO](https://ciso.economictimes.indiatimes.com/amp/news/vulnerabilities-exploits/cert-in-gets-mythos-access-to-assess-its-use-in-measured-manner/135519439)). In parallel, TRAI has proposed guidelines for AI use in telecom networks and services: operators must classify AI systems, maintain an inventory (no system may be deployed unless inventoried), and report AI-related incidents to the regulator, with stakeholder comments open until November 27, 2026 ([TechGig](https://techgig.com/news/governance-policy/trai-proposes-new-ai-guidelines-for-telecom-networks/135447415)). Together they sketch the state's dual posture on AI — aggressive adopter in the security apparatus, cautious rule-writer for the telecom layer that carries everyone else's DPI traffic. The gap to watch: the same incident-reporting discipline TRAI wants from telcos does not yet exist for AI systems inside government itself.

## [L1 · Identity] Children's biometrics: 2 crore updates done, the free window runs to 2027

UIDAI has completed over 2 crore mandatory biometric updates (MBU) for school children under its outreach programme ([DD News](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/uidai-completes-over-2-crore-mandatory-biometric-updates-for-school-children)). MBU — re-capturing fingerprints, iris and photograph at ages 5 and 15 — is the maintenance chore that keeps 145+ crore Aadhaars authentication-viable, and UIDAI has made it free for all children aged 5-17 until September 30, 2027, alongside free document updates for adults until June 14, 2027 ([UIDAI](https://uidai.gov.in)). The child-enrolment push matters downstream: school-linked Aadhaar seeding is the identity substrate for scholarships, mid-day meal DBT and the MBU pipeline itself — every failed update today is an authentication failure in some welfare database years from now.
