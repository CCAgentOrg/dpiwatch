# DPI Brief — August 14, 2026

India's Digital Public Infrastructure ecosystem saw significant developments across payments, governance, health, and trust layers in the past 24 hours. Here are the key updates.

## UPI Enters "UPI@10" Era as Government Signals MDR Flexibility (L2 — Payments)

The Finance Ministry has signalled that subsidies alone cannot sustain UPI's next growth phase as the system enters its tenth year of operations. According to a detailed analysis in [UPSC daily current affairs](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/upsc-mains-current-affairs/2026/08/13), UPI ecosystem costs are estimated around ₹20,000 crore annually, while existing government subsidies do not fully cover technology, infrastructure, and compliance expenses.

The recently-passed [Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/indias-new-tax-amendment-bill-everything-businesses-need-to-know) amends the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, giving the Central Government discretion to determine which digital payment modes shall not incur charges. This effectively creates a flexible framework for future Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) decisions without mandating blanket zero-MDR on all UPI transactions.

Industry proposals favour MDR of 0.3–0.6% on transactions above ₹2,000 involving larger merchants, while keeping small-merchant transactions free — a model that could fund infrastructure without burdening micro-businesses. UPI processed 24,161.69 crore transactions worth ₹314.23 lakh crore in FY 2025-26, representing 85% of India's digital payment volume.

## Supreme Court Asks Centre to Consider AI Ethics Guidelines (L7 — Trust)

The Supreme Court on August 13 asked the Centre and other authorities to consider suggestions on framing binding guidelines for ethics and transparency in AI-based surveillance and content moderation. A bench comprising [Chief Justice of India Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V Mohana](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/info-tech/supreme-court-asks-centre-to-consider-framing-guidelines-on-ai-ethics/article71339983.ece) heard a petition filed by advocate Narendra Kumar Goswami.

While the Court declined to entertain the plea seeking judicial guidelines, it directed the Centre to consider the petitioner's representations on regulating the use of high-risk AI systems in welfare distribution, policing, and content moderation. India currently lacks a comprehensive AI governance law, relying on voluntary MeitY guidelines — a gap that the petitioner argues leaves citizens vulnerable to algorithmic harm.

## PIB Releases Comprehensive DPI Factsheet: 145 Crore Aadhaar, 72 Crore DigiLocker Users (L1, L3 — Identity & Documents)

The [PIB factsheet on "Empowering Governance through Digital Infrastructure"](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?id=150840&NoteId=150840&ModuleId=16) released on August 13 provides updated scale metrics across India's DPI stack:

- **Aadhaar (L1):** Over 145 crore Aadhaar numbers generated, up from 0.42 crore in 2010-11. The UIDAI has launched a new Aadhaar App supporting offline verification and selective data sharing.
- **DigiLocker (L3):** Over 72.43 crore registered users and 934+ crore documents issued as of July 31, 2026. 5,437 document types/services are available (661 Central, 4,776 State).
- **UMANG (L3):** Expanded from 166 services in 2017 to 2,585 services by July 2026, with over 798 crore transactions recorded over the last three years and 11.66 crore registered users.
- **ONDC (L4):** Over 20 crore buyers, 5 lakh sellers, presence in 1,000 cities, and nearly 90 lakh monthly transactions by June 2026.
- **GeM (L4):** Cumulative GMV exceeding ₹18.4 lakh crore, including ₹5 lakh crore in FY 2025-26, enabling over 11 lakh MSMEs.

## Odisha Sets August 31 ABDM Integration Deadline for Empanelled Hospitals (L5 — Sectoral)

The Odisha State Health Assurance Society (SHAS) has directed all private empanelled hospitals to complete integration with the [Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)](https://pragativadi.com/odisha-empaneled-hospitals-ordered-to-set-up-24x7-help-desks-comply-with-abdm-by-aug-31) systems by August 31, 2026. CEO Dr Brundha D. mandated integration of Hospital Management Information Systems (HMIS), ABHA-based patient medical records, Healthcare Facility Registry, and Health Professional Registry.

Hospitals have also been asked to set up 24x7 help desks for Ayushman Bharat cardholders and ensure real-time claim processing through the digital infrastructure. The deadline underscores the state's push toward universal digital health records, but implementation readiness across private hospitals remains a concern.

## Parliamentary Panel Flags BharatNet Rollout Gaps (L6 — Governance)

A Standing Committee on Communications and IT has [flagged significant gaps in BharatNet](https://telecom.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy/parliamentary-panel-flags-gaps-in-bharatnet-rollout-seeks-state-wise-performance-audit/133077324) rollout, noting that only 78,899 out of approximately 2.64 lakh Gram Panchayats had operational Points of Presence as of February 28, 2026 — roughly 30% coverage despite years of deployment.

The panel, reporting on August 10, called for a comprehensive state-wise and model-wise performance audit to identify bottlenecks. The committee noted uneven implementation across states, indicating "gaps in planning, coordination and execution." BharatNet remains the backbone for delivering DPI services to rural India — its underperformance directly impacts access to DigiLocker, UMANG, and other digital governance services in underserved areas.
