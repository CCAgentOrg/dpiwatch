# DPI Brief — November 25, 2026

India's payment system got its most consumer-facing proposal in years: an emergency brake for your bank account. The trust layer kept ringing alarm bells about AI-powered attacks, the welfare state consolidated two dozen portals into one login launching today, and the documents layer quietly crossed new milestones. Here is what matters across the DPI stack.

## 1. RBI proposes a 'Stop Switch' — one tap to freeze all outgoing debits

On 23 November the Reserve Bank of India released [draft amendment directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/bs_viewcontent.aspx?Id=5227) that would hand customers direct, preventive control over their own accounts. The headline feature is a **Stop Switch**: once activated, it blocks all customer-initiated future debits, new payment mandates and standing instructions, and even registration of the account on fresh third-party UPI apps — while incoming credits, bank-recovered dues, liens and already-running mandates keep flowing. Alongside it, banks must offer customer-set transaction limits and channel-wise controls. The draft also proposes a voluntary **Trusted Person Mechanism**: a customer can register another adult (with their own mobile number and verified ID) as a second check on transfers above a self-chosen threshold; the bank waits [24 hours after registration before activating it](https://dailypioneer.com/business/rbi-draft-banks-must-offer-stop-switch-trusted-person-check-to-curb-payment-fraud), and each flagged transfer then needs the trusted person's approval within one hour — no response, no money leaves. The trusted person gets no account access and carries no financial liability. Crucially, the mechanism **excludes UPI, merchant payments and recurring mandates** — so it protects the vulnerable elderly-fraud corridor of NEFT/IMPS/RTGS and cards, not the everyday UPI rail. Covering commercial banks, small finance banks, payments banks, RRBs and cooperative banks, the rules are slated for 1 July 2027 (negligence-related provisions earlier, from 1 January 2027), with [feedback due by 14 December](https://inc42.com/buzz/rbi-proposes-stop-switch-to-block-digital-payment-frauds). Notably, RBI dropped two ideas from its April discussion paper — a blanket one-hour payment delay and a shadow-mode credit ceiling — in favour of customer-controlled tools ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/rbi-proposes-trusted-person-sign-off-electronic-transfers-curb-fraud-2026-11-23); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/stop-switch-payment-limits-rbi-lists-new-safeguards-against-bank-fraud-126112400518_1.html)).

## 2. Welfare DPI consolidation: SAMAVESH and e-Anudaan launch today

Today (25 November) the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment launches **SAMAVESH** — Single Access Mechanism for All Verticals of Empowerment and Social Harmony — and a revamped **e-Anudaan** grant portal at the Dr Ambedkar International Centre in New Delhi, per the [official PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2340843). SAMAVESH folds 20-plus departmental portals behind one common login, adds an AI-powered multilingual chatbot, automated document verification and digital monitoring of institutions receiving government assistance. An MoU with [BHASHINI](https://opengovasia.com/india-launches-unified-welfare-access-and-grant-management-platforms) brings document summarisation and service access in 22-plus Indian languages, and the platform integrates with DigiLocker and PFMS — putting the documents and payments layers of DPI directly inside a welfare gateway. e-Anudaan will handle grant applications and monitoring under four departmental schemes, aimed at the NGOs and institutions that actually deliver the services. The consolidation logic mirrors what UPI did for payments: many interfaces, one pipe.

## 3. CERT-In warns agentic AI attacks have spread beyond banking — and TRAI's AI clock ticks

CERT-In's [20 November advisory](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-cert-in-agentic-ai-financial-cyberattacks-bfsi) reports that AI-driven **agentic offensive frameworks** are now hitting technology service providers handling government-linked payments and vendor/payout platforms — automated reconnaissance, credential attacks and unauthorised access executed by AI agents rather than human operators. This extends the agency's 9 October warning, which flagged stolen API credentials being used to drain NBFCs, fintechs and payment aggregators. The pattern matters for DPI because much of India's payment and welfare plumbing runs through exactly these back-office service providers. Meanwhile in telecom, the Internet Freedom Foundation has [asked TRAI to extend the comment deadline](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2026/11/iff-asks-trai-to-extend-telecom-ai-guidelines-comment-deadline) on its draft AI guidelines for telecom — released 30 October with comments due 27 November and, pointedly, no counter-comment period at all.

## 4. The documents layer: bank guarantees go digital, DigiLocker crosses 700 million

NeSL announced that **electronic bank guarantees** can now be issued into and verified through [NeSL-EntityLocker](https://www.instagram.com/p/DgEm8aZB93S), letting organisations view, share and verify e-BGs digitally without paper or tamper risk — a meaningful slice of enterprise paperwork moving into the DigiLocker ecosystem. The scale of that ecosystem is now [over 700 million users and more than 9 billion issued verifiable credentials](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/india-offers-transformative-growth-story-through-population-scale-digital-infrastructure-dr-chandra-sekhar-pemmasani), per MoS IT-Telecom Dr Pemmasani.

## 5. Briefly noted

- **Identity (L1):** UIDAI's advisory of 20 November makes mandatory biometric updates free for all children aged 5–17 [until 30 September 2027](https://www.babushahi.com/full-news.php?id=230182), urging parents to hit the age milestones — missing them is a leading cause of failed Aadhaar authentication later in scholarship and exam processes.
- **Health (L5):** The Pharmacy Council of India issued a [go-live circular on 24 November](https://pci.gov.in/en/blog/user-manual-for-the-nha-hpr-digi-pharmed-portal) for the NHA HPR DIGI-PHARMED portal, putting pharmacists onto ABDM's Healthcare Professionals Registry.
- **Payments (L2):** The MDR wait drags on — with no rollout date, [fintechs say government incentives have gone unpaid for six quarters](https://bfsi.eletsonline.com/upi-mdr-delay-fintech-incentives), and NPCI is meanwhile talking to Google about hardening Android against UPI fraud.

The through-line today is control moving toward the edge: RBI wants the customer holding the emergency brake, SAMAVESH wants the citizen at one window, and CERT-In wants defenders to assume the attacker is already automated. All three are the right instincts — the test is whether implementation arrives faster than the threats.
