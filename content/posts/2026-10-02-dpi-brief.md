# DPI Brief — October 02, 2026

Window covered: **8:00 a.m. IST on October 1 to 8:00 a.m. IST on October 2.** The clearest signals were fresh UPI activity data and a set of pension-service launches on NPS Diwas. The pension initiatives are downstream applications of digital rails, not new core DPI infrastructure.

## 1. UPI: September set a daily-use record, despite a smaller monthly total

NPCI’s September figures, reported on October 1, show **24.07 billion transactions worth ₹29.37 trillion**. Monthly volume was 1.8% below August’s 24.51 billion, but September had 30 days against August’s 31: average daily use rose to a record **802 million payments**, worth ₹97,913 crore a day. Year on year, volume grew 23% and value 18%, according to [Business Standard’s report of NPCI data](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/upi-transactions-september-2026-mdr-october-15-npci-126100100510_1.html); NPCI publishes the underlying series on its [UPI statistics page](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-statistics).

The distinction matters as the MDR debate intensifies. A lower monthly count is not, by itself, evidence that users are backing away from UPI: September had one fewer day, while average daily use rose. Nor can September data show the effect of a fee that has not started. NPCI’s [MDR FAQ](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/FA_Qs_Merchant_Discount_Rate_MDR_on_Select_UPI_P2_M_Transactions_58dba1d39e.pdf) says the revised charge begins on October 15 for specified merchant payments. The first post-implementation data will be the real test of merchant behaviour and any cost pass-through.

## 2. NPS Tatkal turns UPI into an account-opening route

On NPS Diwas, PFRDA announced **NPS Tatkal**, a route to open a National Pension System account through UPI using a KYC-verified bank account; the initial app is BHIM UPI. PFRDA chairperson S. Ramann told PTI that five banks had joined—SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and IDFC FIRST—and set a target of **2–3 crore additional subscribers over two years**. That number is the regulator’s stated target, not an observed result. [CNBC-TV18’s PTI report](https://www.cnbctv18.com/personal-finance/expect-to-add-nearly-3-crore-pension-subscribers-via-nps-tatkal-in-2-years-pfrda-chief-20003189.htm) says contributions can also be made through UPI.

For the DPI lens, this is a meaningful edge use of Layer 2: a familiar payment interface becomes an entry point to a regulated, long-term financial product. The consumer test is whether the streamlined flow makes charges, contribution rules, investment risk and exit conditions as legible as the enrolment button.

## 3. NPS Sanchay pairs simpler defaults with assisted outreach

PFRDA also launched **NPS Sanchay** and flagged off the first Pension Yatras from Delhi, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru and Kolkata. The four routes will cover districts over 15 days, with 12 more planned over the following six months. The outreach targets people with limited access to pension information, including informal-sector and self-employed workers. [Business Today’s report](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/story/pfrda-launches-nps-sanchay-pension-yatra-on-nps-diwas-2026-what-is-nps-sanchay-and-who-can-open-an-account-559035-2026-10-01) says Sanchay’s initial allocation is 75% government securities and corporate bonds, 25% equities, with the option to change later; [PFRDA’s announcement](https://x.com/PFRDAOfficial/status/2105611617753030885) confirms the launch and four-city flag-off.

This combines digital distribution with in-person explanation rather than assuming an app alone will close the access gap. But a default is still a decision made on a subscriber’s behalf. Outreach should explain the allocation, market risk and how to change it—not only how quickly an account can be opened.

## 4. NPS Swasthya links a pension corpus to health cover

In its [official NPS Diwas announcement](https://x.com/PFRDAOfficial/status/2105611617753030885), PFRDA said **NPS Swasthya** would let subscribers build a dedicated retirement corpus alongside access to super top-up group health insurance, with the pension fund as master policyholder. The regulator also announced a Retirement Income Scheme. Swasthya sits at the boundary of finance and health, but it is not an ABHA or health-record interoperability launch. Before judging the consumer value, the documents that matter are the cover’s exclusions, premium funding, claim process and what happens when a subscriber changes pension arrangements.

## Seven-layer scan

The scan found no new dated official service or policy change in **L1 Aadhaar** (the Aadhaar Samvaad package was announced September 29 and covered in yesterday’s brief), **L3 DigiLocker/eSign, L4 ONDC/GeM, L5 ABHA/AgriStack, L6 CPGRAMS/eOffice or L7 CERT-In/DPDP/TRAI** during this window. L2 had new monthly data and a downstream pension-onboarding use case. The NPS launches should be read as adoption of digital channels—not evidence that any of the other core DPI layers changed overnight.
