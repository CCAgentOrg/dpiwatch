# DPI Brief — November 20, 2026

The biggest story of the past 24 hours is a fee that refuses to arrive: India's payments industry spent Thursday repricing itself around yet another UPI merchant-fee delay, while the trust layer picked up a Supreme Court date in December. Here is what moved across India's Digital Public Infrastructure stack.

## L2 · Payments: UPI merchant fee in limbo, markets react

Shares of [Paytm and Pine Labs fell on Thursday](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/paytm-pine-labs-shares-plunge-upi-transaction-fee-rollout-india-delayed-2026-11-19) after reports that the rollout of the UPI merchant discount rate (MDR) has slipped again, with no new implementation date. NPCI had announced in September a 0.4% fee on specified merchant payments above ₹2,000 (capped at ₹300 per transaction), effective October 15 — then postponed it "until after Diwali." Sources tell [CNBC-TV18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c99VCWpT8Jk) that the timeline remains unresolved between the government, RBI and NPCI, and [Moneycontrol reports](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/upi-mdr-rollout-may-be-delayed-until-after-2027-assembly-elections-report-14084125.html) officials are weighing a delay past the 2027 assembly elections in five states.

Two design details surfaced alongside the dithering. NPCI's steering committee has approved a [flat ₹5 MDR for B2B UPI payments](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/npci-panel-sets-rs5-flat-fee-for-b2b-upi-payments-under-new-mdr-framework-report-568393-2026-11-19), replacing percentage-based charges of up to ₹300 for business-to-business transfers. And a November 11 circular notified the **UPI Vyapaar Saksham scheme** — QR codes and soundboxes for small merchants, funded from MDR collections, running for three years from the day MDR actually begins. The architecture is now fully built; only the start date is missing.

## L2 · Payments: underneath the fee fight, a record month

NPCI's October data shows UPI processed [25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore](https://www.jagranjosh.com/general-knowledge/upi-creates-history-in-october-2026-transactions-cross-3153-lakh-crore-for-the-first-time-1820013888-1) — its highest-ever month on both counts, and the first time monthly value crossed ₹30 lakh crore. Volume grew 23% year-on-year, value 16%, with a daily average of 822 million transactions across 758 live banks. As the [Observer Research Foundation notes](https://www.orfonline.org/expert-speak/upi-at-a-crossroads-a-small-charge-a-large-unknown), September's 1.8% volume dip ahead of the planned fee start and October's record rebound show how hard it is to attribute movements — which is exactly why the fee's actual demand effects remain unknowable until it launches.

## L7 · Trust: DPDP challenge gets a December date

The Supreme Court [adjourned to December 9](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/sc-adjourns-hearing-on-dpdp-act-rti-amendments-to-december-9-160384.html) the hearing on petitions challenging the DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, without granting an interim stay. Petitioners include journalists, activists, and the Digital News Publishers Association representing 21 outlets (Network18, NDTV, The Indian Express, Times Internet among them). Two flashpoints: Section 44(3)'s amendment of the RTI Act's Section 8(1)(j), which removes the "larger public interest" test for disclosing personal information; and the absence of a journalism exemption — one that existed in the 2018, 2019 and 2021 draft bills and was dropped in 2022. December 9 is now a date the entire transparency ecosystem should circle.

## L1 · Identity: courts and exams both shrink Aadhaar's DOB claims

The Delhi High Court [reiterated this week](https://www.livelaw.in/high-court/delhi-high-court/aadhaar-card-by-itself-not-proof-of-date-of-birth-delhi-high-court-559148) that an Aadhaar card is not, by itself, proof of date of birth — dismissing an employer's bid to retire a worker a year early by leaning on her Aadhaar-recorded DOB over an earlier ESIC record. Justice Manoj Kumar Ohri relied on UIDAI's own office memorandum of December 20, 2018, and the Supreme Court's *Saroj v. IFFCO-Tokio* ruling. It follows the [Madhya Pradesh High Court's November 11 decision](https://www.gktoday.in/madhya-pradesh-high-court-rejects-aadhaar-as-conclusive-age-proof) rejecting Aadhaar as conclusive proof of age in a motor-accident compensation case. The courts are simply enforcing what UIDAI already says in writing: Aadhaar proves identity, not birthdate.

Separately, the National Testing Agency's [November 17 advisory](https://www.shiksha.com/news/medicine-health-sciences-nta-advises-students-to-update-aadhaar-biometrics-before-neet-ug-2027-registration-check-who-needs-it-blogId-246990) asks NEET UG 2027 candidates to update Aadhaar biometrics before registration — a reminder that UIDAI's free mandatory biometric updates at ages 5 and 15 have quietly become exam-gating infrastructure.

## L4 · Commerce: tribal products get an ONDC on-ramp

TRIFED met [ONDC on November 18](https://www.facebook.com/tribesindia/posts/1460470149594477) at its New Delhi headquarters to take Tribes India products onto the open network — extending the DPI commerce story to tribal artisan supply chains. The network's financial-services arm is also widening: [ONDC executives say](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tGu1EzDUTgQ) credit cards, loans against mutual funds and BNPL are next in the pipeline, while festive seller DigiHaat reported [175.6% growth in non-food orders](https://indianretailer.com/news/digihaat-records-1756-pc-growth-festive-non-food-orders-ondc-network) on the network this season.

## Layer watch

- **L5 · Sectoral:** A multi-state [time-motion study in BMC Health Services Research](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-026-16042-4) found ABDM's Scan and Share service measurably cuts OPD registration wait times in public hospitals; the stack now counts ~96 crore ABHA IDs with 100+ crore linked records. The National Health Authority's [November 5–6 national review in Kolkata](https://www.bwhealthcareworld.com/article/nha-reviews-ayushman-bharat-schemes-plans-roadmap-to-strengthen-healthcare-delivery-and-digital-integration-633399) set a roadmap for deeper digital integration across states.
- **L6 · Governance:** DARPG released its [CPGRAMS monthly report for October](https://pgportal.gov.in), with daily disposals exceeding 8,600 grievances in early November; the Controller General of Accounts issued a [November 18 order](https://cga.nic.in/Circular/Published/10622.aspx) mandating daily CPGRAMS monitoring per DARPG's standing instructions — grievance redressal discipline is being pushed down to the accounting establishment.

**Watch item:** December 9 (Supreme Court DPDP hearing), and whatever date NPCI finally attaches to the MDR.
