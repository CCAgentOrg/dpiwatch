# DPI Brief — November 11, 2026

**Top DPI headlines for November 11, 2026** — UPI sets a Diwali single-day record while the merchant-charge notification stays missing, Ayushman Bharat puts AI in the claims-adjudication seat, DPDP phase two lands Friday, and MeitY admits AI-agent intrusions went undetected for months.

## [L2 · Payments] Diwali delivered UPI's biggest single day — and the MDR clock still isn't running

Diwali (November 8) saw UPI process **91.69 crore transactions**, up 32% from 69.57 crore a year earlier, worth ₹90,029 crore ([PTI/The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/business/upi-volume-jumps-32-per-cent-on-diwali-262459), [BW Businessworld](https://www.businessworld.in/article/upi-transactions-rise-32-on-diwali-as-festival-payments-cross-916-mn-632991)). Dhanteras-eve was bigger still: 97.58 crore transactions worth ₹1.36 lakh crore on November 7 — the festive peak. The run caps October's record month of 25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore, and a decade in which UPI's annual value grew from ₹7,000 crore in FY17 to roughly ₹314 lakh crore in FY26. The uncomfortable pairing: with Diwali and Bhai Dooj (today) behind it, NPCI has still not notified an implementation date for the MDR framework on person-to-merchant payments above ₹2,000 — originally slated for October 15, deferred on October 13 "until after Diwali" ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/economy/story/upi-payments-above-rs2000-to-attract-0-4-mdr-after-diwali-who-pays-who-is-exempt-and-when-charges-begin-566737-2026-11-10)). Merchant bodies are asking for the revised date ([Orissa Post](https://www.orissapost.com/merchants-seek-clarity-on-upi-mdr-rollout-after-diwali/amp)); reporting suggests a mid-November rollout, with a 0.4% charge capped (₹300 on a ₹1 lakh payment), a concessional flat ₹5 for railways, telecom, insurance and fuel, and the standing exemptions — payments up to ₹2,000, RuPay debit cards, small merchants under ₹1 lakh monthly inflow — untouched, with no pass-through to customers permitted ([Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/upi-charges-after-diwali-will-customers-pay-extra-on-payments-above-2-000-npcis-mdr-rollout-likely-in-nov-11794289795387.html)). A record month is exactly when consumers are least equipped to notice a charge appearing at the till; read the eventual notification against November's baseline.

## [L5 · Sectoral] Ayushman Bharat hands claims adjudication to AI; Tripura's hospitals go paperless today

The government is planning an AI overhaul of Ayushman Bharat's operational core — **automated claims adjudication** to cut processing time and cost, AI-enabled clinical documentation, fraud detection and data analytics across PM-JAY and ABDM ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/ayushman-bharat-set-for-ai-overhaul-faster-claims-fraud-detection-and-digital-medical-records-566641-2026-11-10)). It is the concrete follow-through to last week's national review in Kolkata, which named hospital-system integration, AI and cybersecurity the mission's next-phase priorities. Auto-adjudication is the consequential piece: an algorithm deciding which ₹5-lakh hospitalisation claims get paid shifts the dispute from doctor-versus-insurer to citizen-versus-model — and the readout so far says nothing about appeal rights or audit trails for rejected claims. Meanwhile the paperless frontier is now real: all government hospitals in **Tripura** switched to fully paperless operations from **November 10** — OPD registration, prescriptions, dispensing and lab tests all digital under ABDM ([Digital Health News](https://www.digitalhealthnews.com/tripura-showcases-digital-healthcare-model-at-national-abdm-ab-pmjay-review-meeting)). An entire state's public hospital system writing into the health records stack is exactly the data-quality test the NHA review flagged.

## [L7 · Trust] DPDP phase two bites Friday — India Inc is still pricing consent

The second phase of DPDP Act obligations begins **November 13**, covering consent mechanisms, retention limits, breach-notification readiness and data-principal rights ([India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-it-act-dpdp-act-ai-compliance-47725.html)). With two days to go, CFOs report grappling with compliance costs, consent-management plumbing and third-party supply-chain risk — the parts of readiness that sit outside the CISO's remit ([ET CFO](https://cfo.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/cfo-tech/dpdp-compliance-cfos-grapple-with-costs-consent-management-and-supply-chain-risks/135672678)). Significant Data Fiduciaries additionally owe annual impact assessments and audits under Rule 13. The journalism carve-out fight continues on parallel tracks: government sources' informal assurance that newsrooms aren't fiduciaries, and the Digital News Publishers Association's Supreme Court challenge next listed November 17. An exemption that lives in goodwill is not an exemption.

## [L7 × L2 · Trust meets Payments] MeitY: AI-agent intrusions on Indian organisations went undetected for months

A senior MeitY official said **Anthropic and OpenAI learned of AI agents' unauthorised access to Indian organisations' systems "much later"** and reported it to CERT-In only after months had passed — and the affected organisations never detected the intrusions themselves ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/tech/ai-firms-took-months-to-detect-agents-in-indian-systems-cert-in-now-has-mythos-access-meity-official/3089934)). The disclosure lands in a busy fortnight for the trust layer: CERT-In's AI Security Auditing Guidelines (November 4) with a four-tier risk framework ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-lowdown-cert-in-ai-security-auditing-guidelines)), controlled access to Anthropic's frontier Mythos model for vulnerability testing ([ETGovernment](http://government.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/technology/cert-in-gets-mythos-access-to-assess-its-use-in-measured-manner-it-ministry-sources/135523490)), an advisory naming threat actor "CRAFTY FALCON" using ₹1 transfers to test fund flows before larger raids, and RBI pushing security audits as AI-assisted attacks on banks, NBFCs and payment aggregators rise ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-rbi-bankers-ai-cyber-attacks-security-audits)). The connective tissue is agentic AI meeting India's payment rails — NPCI is separately building a registry to identify AI agents that initiate UPI transactions ([TechNode Global](https://technode.global/2026/11/09/npci-launches-tech-solutions-subsidiary)). Machines are now both the attacker and the counterparty; neither the detection stack nor the KYC stack was designed for that.
