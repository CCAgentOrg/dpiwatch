# DPI Brief — August 29, 2026

## L1: Digital India Completes 11 Years; e-Shram Portal Turns Five

Prime Minister Narendra Modi marked 11 years of the [Digital India programme](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/digital-india-redefined-governance-empowered-citizens-accelerated-development-pm-modi-hails-11-years-of-flagship-initiative) on August 28, describing it as the cornerstone of a "developed India" — a digital governance framework that has evolved from connectivity rollout to a layered DPI stack.

The anniversary coincided with a structurally significant milestone: the [e-Shram portal](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2303053&reg=3&lang=1) completed five years on August 26. Built by the National Informatics Centre, e-Shram is India's first Aadhaar-authenticated National Database of Unorganised Workers. It assigns every registered worker a 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN), layering L1 identity infrastructure directly onto social security delivery. The platform covers agricultural labourers, construction workers, domestic workers, gig workers, and street vendors — enabling welfare-scheme convergence and benefit portability across states. Five years in, it underscores how Aadhaar authentication has moved beyond simple KYC into programme administration for India's vast informal economy.

## L2: Allahabad HC Notices RBI, NPCI on UPI "Accept/Decline" Plea

The [Allahabad High Court](https://www.medianama.com/2026/08/223-allahabad-high-court-digital-payments) (Lucknow Bench) issued notices to the RBI and NPCI on a PIL seeking an "accept or decline" option for incoming electronic payments. A Division Bench of Justice Rajan Roy and Justice Manjive Shukla heard the petition filed by advocate Anupriya Agarwal on August 24. The Union Ministry of Finance is also a respondent.

The case targets a real friction point: UPI's instant push architecture means money lands before the recipient can object. For fraud victims — where suspicious credits are used to implicate innocent account holders — the absence of a consent gate is a genuine vulnerability. If the court mandates an accept/decline layer, it would be the most significant product-level change to UPI since the introduction of the 4-hour reversal window.

Separately, [digital payments to merchants](https://businessnewsthisweek.com/business/digital-payments-to-merchants-rise-19-6-to-rs-11-7-trillion-in-july) rose 19.6% year-on-year to approximately ₹11.7 trillion in July 2026, reflecting sustained merchant onboarding and consumption momentum.

## L5: BRICS Digital Agriculture Network; ABDM Crosses 110 Crore Health Records

India's 2026 BRICS chairship has produced 20 major initiatives, and two are directly relevant to the DPI stack. A [BRICS Network on Digital Agriculture](https://newstodaynet.com/2026/08/28/startup-fund-digital-agriculture-among-key-brics-outcomes/) will leverage AI, geospatial technology, and digital infrastructure to promote data-driven farming across member nations — effectively exporting India's AgriStack model to a multilateral framework. Member countries also agreed on a Network of Centres of Excellence on Agro-Ecology and Regenerative Agriculture, complementing the technology-first approach with ecological sustainability.

On the health stack, the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission has [digitally linked over 110 crore health records](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?Id=150905&reg=3&lang=+2) as of August 12. Women hold 49.72% of ABHA accounts. Over 45.5 crore Ayushman cards have been created under AB-PMJAY, with 22.48 crore issued to women and more than 5.25 crore hospital admissions being of women — making gender equity a measurable outcome of the digital health stack.

## L6/L7: DPDP Compliance Push, PM KUSUM Digital Platform, CERT-In State Exercise

Cabinet Secretary T.V. Somanathan has directed all central ministries and state governments to expedite compliance with the [Digital Personal Data Protection Act](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dcktaa7lp5A), signalling impatience with the pace of institutional readiness. The directive targets both Union and state-level data fiduciaries, reminding them that the Act's obligations are not optional.

SECI has floated a [tender for a National PM KUSUM Digital Platform](https://www.seci.co.in) covering design, development, deployment, migration, integration, monitoring, and five-year O&M. The platform will digitise the entire PM-KUSUM solar pump subsidy lifecycle — from farmer registration to installation verification and subsidy disbursement — moving a ₹75,000 crore programme from paper-based processing to a managed digital workflow.

CERT-In Director General Sanjay Bahl led a two-day [cybersecurity capacity-building exercise](https://www.facebook.com/nemediahub/posts/a-cybersecurity-debate-in-the-meghalaya-assembly-saw-discussions-ranging-from-gl/1523581193142118) at the Meghalaya Assembly, focused on strengthening state-level cyber readiness. The event comes amid the Union government's broader push to decentralise cybersecurity capabilities to state legislatures and departments.
