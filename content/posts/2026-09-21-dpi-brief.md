# DPI Brief — September 21, 2026

**Aadhaar authentication becomes the gate to subsidised LPG (L1 — Identity).** The Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas has made Biometric Aadhaar Authentication (BAA) compulsory for domestic LPG consumers who want to book refills at the regulated retail selling price with subsidy, effective **October 1, 2026**. Consumers who skip authentication will pay the non-subsidised price — [no Aadhaar biometric authentication, no subsidised refill](https://www.thestatesman.com/business/lpg-subsidy-rules-change-from-october-1-no-aadhaar-biometric-authentication-no-subsidised-refill-1503640642.html), as the rule is being reported. [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/aadhaar-authentication-for-lpg-subsidy-what-consumers-must-do-before-october-1) walks through what households must do before the deadline. This moves Aadhaar authentication from subsidy-transfer (the PAHAL/DBTL model) to the point of sale itself, on a base of over 31 crore LPG connections. The compliance burden falls on the consumer: anyone with stale Aadhaar-linked details must re-verify within nine days.

**NPCI seeds a small-merchant fund out of UPI's new MDR pot (L2 — Payments).** With the 0.4% MDR on select UPI person-to-merchant transactions above ₹2,000 taking effect October 15, [The Economic Times reports](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/policy/upis-new-mdr-pot-5-of-collections-to-be-used-for-bringing-small-merchants-online/articleshow/134364457.cms) that NPCI has proposed a dedicated fund financed by **5% of total MDR collections**, to pay ecosystem players for small-merchant onboarding and to build digital payment infrastructure in Tier 3–6 locations — explicitly including the Northeast, Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. The framework's guardrails remain: merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh/month via UPI QR stay at zero MDR, transactions up to ₹2,000 stay free, and the [Finance Ministry says ~96% of P2M transactions are unaffected](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/upi-mdr-what-changes-from-october-15-heres-what-merchants-and-consumers-need-to-know/134275850). NPCI also launched **MyUPI**, an AI-powered support service bundling transaction help, safety tools and Tap & Pay — arriving just as the MDR rules are generating a support-load of their own. The revenue conversation sits on top of record scale: UPI processed **24.51 billion transactions worth ₹29.82 lakh crore in August** (791 million/day), [its highest-ever monthly volume](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/economy/upi-sets-new-record-with-24-51-billion-transactions-in-august-11985650).

**IndianOil becomes the first PSU to issue appointment letters on DigiLocker (L3 — Documents).** In collaboration with MeitY, Indian Oil Corporation has become the first public sector undertaking to issue digital Offers of Appointment through DigiLocker — verified, paperless recruitment documents accessible anywhere. [DigiLocker's official account](https://x.com/digilocker_ind/status/2100198791811125403) called it "a new milestone in India's journey towards paperless employment." The consumer-protection angle is real: forged appointment letters are a recurring job-scam vector, and issuer-signed digital documents in a locker tied to verified identity raise the cost of fakes considerably.

**ABHA becomes the spine of Karnataka's health records — and maybe its blood banks (L5 — Sectoral).** [Karnataka will roll out statewide digital health records from early next year](https://bangaloremirror.indiatimes.com/bangalore/others/state-plans-digital-health-records-rollout-from-early-next-year/articleshow/134318073.cms), integrating ABDM's Hospital Management System and Community Health System, with patients' **ABHA numbers as their unique digital health identity** — QR-code check-ins at government hospitals and consent-based access to prior diagnoses, prescriptions and investigations. Separately, the [Drugs Technical Advisory Board (94th meeting, August 27)](https://thesouthfirst.com/health/government-considering-system-to-track-blood-donations-through-aadhaar-authentication-of-donors) has recommended amending the Drugs Rules, 1945 to mandate blood-centre registration on e-RaktKosh and **donor authentication via ABHA or Aadhaar-linked OTP** for traceability and follow-up. Two more hooks into the health DPI in one week; the consent architecture is what will decide whether these feel like empowerment or enrolment.

**DARPG launches Special Campaign 6; CPGRAMS grinds on (L6 — Governance).** Dr Jitendra Singh launched the **[Special Campaign 6 web-portal on September 16](https://plutusias.com/union-minister-to-launch-special-campaign-6-web-portal-on-16-sept-2026)**, with a preparatory phase from September 16–30 and implementation October 2–31, targeting institutionalised swachhata, file-pendency reduction and e-waste disposal across government offices. [DARPG's own channels](https://x.com/DARPG_GoI) confirm the campaign's e-waste focus and timelines. Meanwhile CPGRAMS continues its daily churn — [13,557 grievances disposed across Central Ministries and States/UTs on September 17 alone](https://www.instagram.com/darpggol).

**TRAI rewrites the spam rulebook with AI/ML enforcement (L7 — Trust).** On September 18, TRAI notified the **[Telecom Commercial Communication Customer Preference (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026](https://telecomlead.com/telecom-services/trai-tightens-spam-call-rules-with-ai-ml-detection-%E2%82%B90-05-a2p-charge-and-faster-enforcement-127804)**, its biggest UCC overhaul since 2018. The salient features per [TRAI's announcement](https://indiaeducationdiary.in/trai-strengthens-framework-for-curbing-unsolicited-commercial-communications-through-technology-driven-enforcement-and-enhanced-consumer-protection): telecom operators must deploy **AI/ML-based detection of suspected spam** and share intelligence on suspect senders (new Regulation 21A, operationalising a February direction); entities using A2P calls — robocalls, pre-recorded and AI-generated voice calls — must pre-declare their usage and Calling Line Identities, with undeclared calls treated as unsolicited commercial communication; a **[termination charge of up to ₹0.05/minute](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/telecom/telecom-news/trai-mandates-ai-based-spam-detection-imposes-charge-on-robocalls-to-curb-unsolicited-calls/articleshow/134342102.cms)** on A2P calls (exempting TRAI-designated numbering series and authorised calls); graded action against repeat offenders including KYC re-verification and disconnection; and a **consumer appeal mechanism** against complaint resolutions. The consultation began March 13, 2026, with an Open House Discussion on June 3 — the enforcement teeth arrive before the festive season's spam peak.

**The week ahead:** the LPG Aadhaar deadline (October 1) and UPI's MDR switch (October 15) both land within the next month — expect a consumer-confusion cycle around each.
