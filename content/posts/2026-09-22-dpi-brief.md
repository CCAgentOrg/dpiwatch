# DPI Brief — September 22, 2026

**MDR pushback begins: Madhya Pradesh's 4,700 petrol pumps will refuse UPI above ₹2,000 (L2 — Payments).** The new UPI Merchant Discount Rate framework's first consumer-facing fracture has appeared in fuel retail. The [Madhya Pradesh Petroleum Dealers Association has announced](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/no-upi-above-rs-2000-mp-petrol-pumps-to-stop-accepting-payments-from-october-16-over-mdr/articleshow/134387152.cms) that its ~4,700 members will stop accepting UPI payments above ₹2,000 from October 16 — a day after the [0.4% MDR takes effect](https://payu.in/blog/upi-mdr-2026-what-the-new-charge-means-for-businesses) — arguing that thin fuel-retail margins cannot absorb the charge. Association president Ajay Singh told ANI each pump handles roughly 100 daily transactions above ₹2,000, a hit of about ₹590 per day, or [₹17,700 a month and ₹8.17 crore statewide annually](https://www.freepressjournal.in/bhopal/upi-fees-petrol-pumps-refuse-to-accept-upi-payments-above-2k-across-madhya-pradesh-owing-to-thin-margins). Debit and credit cards stay available, and the association has asked the government to extend to UPI the [MDR exemption fuel retailers already enjoy on cards](https://m.economictimes.com/news/new-updates/petrol-pump-upi-payments-above-rs-2000-to-stop-from-october-16-in-madhya-pradesh-dealers-cite-0-4-mdr-charge-debit-and-credit-cards-to-remain-available/articleshow/134355172.cms).

The economics critique is widening beyond one state. Former NITI Aayog vice-chairman Rajiv Kumar [warned the levy "could reverse digital payments gains" and push users back to cash](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/banking/finance/upi-charges-could-reverse-digital-payments-gains-push-users-back-to-cash-says-ex-niti-vc-rajiv-kumar/articleshow/134386120.cms). Legal analysis is surfacing a second-order effect the government's messaging skipped: since [Notification S.O. 5067(E) protects only RuPay debit and UPI up to ₹2,000](https://vinodkothari.com/2026/09/zero-to-mdr-will-lenders-face-a-new-cost-on-loan-repayments), UPI-based loan EMIs — where the lender is the payee — may now attract MDR, with the ₹300 cap applying per EMI transaction rather than per loan. One carve-out is designed to keep money in markets: [payments to mutual funds, securities brokers and dealers carry a reduced 0.02% MDR](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/npci-introduces-mdr-on-high-value-merchant-upi-transactions), capped at ₹300. Compliance trackers also report NPCI has issued a [consolidated UPI circular](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/pc-agrawal-78569614_upi-consolidated-circular-1892026-activity-7507737265989840896-wm9P) restating the ecosystem's rules in a single document dated September 18.

**The LPG eKYC cliff gets sharper: no 14.2 kg refills for non-verified households (L1 — Identity).** The petroleum ministry has clarified the consequences of missing the October 1 Biometric Aadhaar Authentication deadline: households that skip eKYC will not merely forgo the subsidy — they will be supplied [only 5 kg or 10 kg refills at market prices, with the standard 14.2 kg cylinder off the table](https://www.thehindu.com/business/Economy/after-october-1-only-smaller-5-or-10-kg-lpg-refills-await-non-ekyc-households/article71485908.ece). Since 5 and 10 kg cylinders are free-trade products priced like commercial LPG, non-verified households end up paying more for less gas. Roughly [89.9% of households have completed e-KYC](https://www.goodreturns.in/news/lpg-biometric-aadhaar-authentication-october-1-rule-lpg-subsidy-steps-for-ekyc-indane-hp-bharat-gas-1536047.html) after seven deadline extensions between June 30 and September 14. The squeeze is two-sided: the same distributor network now plans to [approach the government over the MDR it faces](https://www.thehindu.com/business/Economy/after-october-1-only-smaller-5-or-10-kg-lpg-refills-await-non-ekyc-households/article71485908.ece) on large-value cylinder payments — identity verification and payment fees converging on one supply chain in the same fortnight. Meanwhile, the central KYC registry's own upgrade got more lab time: [CKYCRR 2.0's pilot-testing period is extended to October 5](https://www.ricago.com/regulatory-updates).

**GATE 2027 makes DigiLocker the mandatory front door to India's biggest exam (L3 — Documents).** IIT Madras has made [DigiLocker registration mandatory for Indian nationals applying to GATE 2027](https://engineering.careers360.com/articles/gate-2027-registration-online-link), with the no-late-fee window closing September 27 — meaning lakhs of candidates must hold a locker account to sit one of the country's largest examinations. Adoption at this scale exposes the friction: a wave of ["DigiLocker verification failed" complaints](https://www.shiksha.com/engineering/articles/digilocker-verification-failed-for-gate-blogId-240945) has produced official fixes (grant the folder-consent, create a GATE2027 folder, keep name/DOB/mobile/email/photo/address and verified ID current). IIT Madras has since [allowed unverified DigiLocker accounts to proceed with a default ID proof](https://www.ndtv.com/education/graduate-aptitude-test-in-engineering-2027-registration-check-last-date-to-apply-new-guidelines-12071111), with documents verified after the window closes. The exam becomes DigiLocker's largest single forced-onboarding event yet — a stress test of verification capacity arriving exactly at the deadline.

**ONDC's open logistics network adds an EV fleet (L4 — Commerce).** Electric mobility company Bounce has joined ONDC's [Open Delivery Protocol, putting its EV fleet onto the open logistics network](https://mediabrief.com/bounce-joins-ondcs-open-delivery-protocol) for last-mile delivery. The protocol's pitch is integration-once-serve-many: merchants reach multiple logistics providers through a single connection instead of one-on-one integrations, while small fleet owners compete for demand alongside national players. ONDC says the logistics network now counts [50+ logistics service providers, 60,000+ merchants, 150,000+ delivery partners and 12,000+ daily deliveries](https://bwautoworld.com/article/bounce-partners-with-ondc-to-expand-ev-based-delivery-network-623902). As [platform-versus-agent standoffs play out abroad](https://www.oquilia.com/news/amazon-blocks-meta-muse-shopping-agent), open rails like ODP are quietly becoming India's answer to who gets to intermediate commerce.

**ABHA's app wall: who gets left behind at the hospital gate (L5 — Sectoral).** A Times of India ground report on [digital-first hospital queues](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/healthcare-goes-digital-when-doctors-go-behind-the-app-wall-who-gets-left-behind/articleshow/134369357.cms) documents the human cost of token generation moving to apps: patients without smartphones or reliable networks arrive around midnight to hold a physical place in line because they cannot generate a digital token, while app users register from home around 5 am. The report lands the same week the National Health Authority [concluded its Chintan Shivir charting Ayushman Bharat's next phase](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/nha-charts-next-phase-of-ayushman-bharat-reforms-stresses-digital-health-adoption-and-financial-sustainability) around digital adoption and financial sustainability — with [over 43.5 crore Ayushman cards issued](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/over-43-5-crore-ayushman-cards-issued-digital-health-ecosystem-expands-rapidly-govt). The gap between enrollment scale and last-mile access is the mission's live stress point.

**Week ahead:** the LPG Aadhaar gate (October 1) and UPI's MDR switch (October 15) are now nine and twenty-three days out; GATE's DigiLocker deadline is five.
