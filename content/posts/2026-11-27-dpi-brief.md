# DPI Brief — November 27, 2026

Old entitlements are getting new rails this week: provident fund money preparing to ride UPI out of EPFO's claims queue, farmer registries being publicly firewalled from the right to sell paddy, and a grievance dashboard crowning a ministry desk that won't publish its own scoreboard. Here is what matters across the stack today.

## 1. EPFO 3.0: PF withdrawals head for UPI and ATMs

India's retirement fund could let members withdraw eligible provident fund money through UPI and ATMs as early as December 2026, with the full EPFO 3.0 platform expected to be announced at the Central Board of Trustees' next meeting in February 2027 — [Business Standard, Nov 26](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/epfo-3-0-launch-in-february-2027-changes-that-epf-subscribers-should-know-126112600471_1.html). The technology is reportedly ready, with final validation trials under way with 100 participants drawn from different parts of the country; withdrawals are expected through the BHIM app, showing available balance, withdrawable amount and the minimum balance that must stay ([Times Now](https://www.timesnownews.com/business-economy/personal-finance/epfo-3-0-explained-upi-withdrawals-atm-facility-new-pf-rules-and-other-major-changes-article-156564625)). Separately, the CBT's 240th meeting on November 19 approved a Systematic Withdrawal Plan for retiring members — monthly, quarterly or half-yearly payouts, with the unwithdrawn corpus continuing to earn interest and the nominee collecting the balance on death ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/epfo-3-0-launch-by-february-2027-from-upi-withdrawals-to-retirement-swp-6-key-changes-pf-members-should-know-about-11795587645424.html)). The new platform is being designed to handle around 200 crore members over the next 50 years, against roughly 36 crore active members today. Instant access is genuinely welcome after years of claim-rejection complaints — but the fine print that decides how much is actually "withdrawable" versus locked as minimum balance is where members will need honesty, not just speed.

## 2. AgriStack crosses 10.3 crore Farmer IDs — and states insist the ID still isn't a toll booth

Maharashtra has issued digital Farmer IDs to 1.44 crore farmers under AgriStack, ranking second nationally ([Daily Excelsior, Nov 26](https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/1-44-crore-farmer-ids-issued-in-maharashtra-under-agristack-second-highest-in-country)). Nationally, more than 10.3 crore Farmer IDs now exist against a target of 11 crore by March 2027 ([AgroSpectrum](https://agrospectrumindia.com/features/67/35171/agristack-ten-questions-before-you-plug-in.html)). The friction point surfaced in Odisha, where the food supplies minister told the assembly that AgriStack registration has **not** been made a mandatory precondition for paddy procurement in the current kharif season, after opposition legislators raised alleged farmer unrest and mandi delays; 22.27 lakh farmers are registered this season, and procurement runs until March 31, 2027 ([Times of India, Bhubaneswar, Nov 26](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bhubaneswar/agristack-id-not-mandatory-to-sell-paddy-minister/articleshow/136167236.cms)). When a minister must publicly deny that a digital ID gates the right to sell, that tells you exactly where adoption friction sits. A farmer registry is only public infrastructure if the analogue right — walking into a mandi and selling — survives untouched without it.

## 3. CPGRAMS: the Finance Ministry's pension desk tops the grievance index, without publishing its numbers

The Department of Financial Services' Pension Reforms Division ranked first in Group B (ministries handling fewer than 500 grievances a month) in DARPG's Grievance Redressal Assessment and Index for October 2026 ([The Hindu BusinessLine, Nov 26](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/policy/finance-ministry-strengthens-pension-grievance-redressal-with-senior-level-monthly-reviews/article71747711.ece)). The division runs a dedicated cell that monitors CPGRAMS daily, and the DFS Secretary has personally reviewed 20 randomly selected closed grievances every month since January 2024. What the report does not disclose: grievance volumes, average disposal time, or how the score was computed against peers. GRAI exists precisely to make grievance redressal comparable across government; a first-rank announcement without the underlying numbers is a scoreboard with the score scratched out. Pensioners — the users here — deserve the raw counts as much as the rank.

## 4. Briefly noted

- **Payments (L2):** SBI scheduled maintenance in the early hours of November 27: UPI down 01:15–02:00 IST (UPI Lite stays available), internet banking/YONO/IMPS 01:15–01:45, RTGS 00:45–01:30, NEFT 01:55–02:40, ATMs 01:45–02:45 ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/sbi-customers-to-face-temporary-disruption-in-upi-atm-internet-banking-services-on-nov-27)).
- **Trust (L7):** Comments on TRAI's draft AI guidelines for telecom close today, November 27. Internet Freedom Foundation has asked for a three-week extension to December 18 and a counter-comment round, noting the draft obligates companies internally but gives users no notice of automated decisions and no way to contest them ([IFF](https://internetfreedom.in/four-weeks-is-not-enough-iff-writes-to-trai-on-telecom-ai-guidelines)).
- **Identity (L1):** UIDAI has opened 17 new Aadhaar Seva Kendras across Haryana — including Gurgaon, Rohtak and Hisar — so routine enrolment and biometric updates no longer require a trip to Chandigarh; fresh enrolment is free ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/uidai-opens-17-aadhaar-seva-kendras-across-haryana-no-need-to-travel-to-chandigarh-for-routine-work)).

The through-line is entitlements meeting rails: PF savings preparing to move over UPI, farmer data feeding procurement desks that must swear the ID isn't mandatory, and citizen complaints rolling up an index that ranks without revealing. Each upgrade speeds up access; each also relocates the chokepoint from a counter to code. When the rail becomes the rule, the consumer question stays the same — who audits it, and can you still get your money, your paddy, or your pension without it?
