# DPI Brief — November 24, 2026

The payments layer owned the day. NPCI softened its merchant-fee plan for businesses, revealed where the new MDR money will actually go, and UPI closed its biggest month ever. The trust layer got its first working gear — India's consent managers are now live — while the commerce network kept courting tribal India and UIDAI kept pushing children's biometric updates. Here is what matters.

## 1. MDR gets gentler for business: flat ₹5 for merchant-to-merchant payments

NPCI's UPI and services steering committee has decided that merchant-to-merchant (M2M) transactions — wholesaler-to-retailer payments, B2B marketplaces and commercial collections — will attract a **flat ₹5 fee** under the revised merchant discount rate framework, against the up-to-₹300 charge businesses had feared, according to [Business Standard (18 November)](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/banking/flat-5-mdr-fee-likely-for-b2b-upi-payments-against-feared-300-charge-126111801534_1.html) and [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/money/upi-fee-shock-eased-b2b-transactions-may-now-attract-just-5-charge-under-revised-mdr-plan-11795088050504.html). The maths explains the relief: average M2M ticket sizes run ₹20,000–₹40,000, so the original 0.4% rate (capped at ₹300 above ₹75,000) would have cost ₹80–₹160 per transfer. The All India Consumer Products Distribution Federation had petitioned the finance minister for an exemption, saying retailers receive roughly 65% of consumer payments over UPI. The catch: the decision is not formally notified and **no implementation date exists** — the rollout announced in September for 15 October was deferred until after Diwali, and the [Payments Council of India has made multiple representations](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/payment-firms-seek-clarity-on-mdr-rollout-for-select-upi-transactions/articleshow/135939899.cms) to the finance ministry and NPCI seeking the final date. [Reuters reported on 19 November](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/paytm-pine-labs-shares-plunge-upi-transaction-fee-rollout-india-delayed-2026-11-19) that Paytm and Pine Labs shares plunged on the delay. Context for the levy itself: NPCI chief executive Dilip Asbe has pegged the ecosystem's annual operating cost at around ₹21,000 crore, with the proposed levy expected to recover ₹13,000–₹15,000 crore in year one. RuPay credit cards linked to UPI are expected to stay **outside** MDR scope for now, with a final decision pending.

## 2. The MDR money already has a destination: Vyapaar Saksham

A [NPCI circular dated 11 November](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/npci-introduces-upi-vyapaar-saksham-scheme-to-boost-digital-payments-among-small-merchants-offers-incentives-for-qr-codes-and-soundboxes/135771249) (detailed reporting followed on 22 November) unveiled the three-year **UPI Vyapaar Saksham Scheme**, targeting small merchants with turnover up to ₹40 lakh in Tier 3–6 centres, the North-East, Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. The fund is built from **5% of the MDR earnings** of issuing banks, acquirers, payer PSPs and UPI apps — meaning the contested merchant fee now visibly funds acceptance infrastructure. Incentives: ₹300 per physical QR code and ₹750 per soundbox deployed, with the fund split 30% for new-merchant onboarding, 50% for transaction growth and 20% for incremental growth. NPCI expects 60–100 lakh new QR codes and 5–10 lakh soundboxes a year, plus 30–50% annual transaction growth among existing small merchants ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/money/npcis-upi-vyapaar-saksham-scheme-to-boost-digital-payments-help-small-merchants-with-incentives-all-we-know-11795165732819.html)).

## 3. UPI's biggest month ever — and the top four reshuffle

October was UPI's record month: **25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore**, the first time monthly value crossed ₹30 lakh crore, with volume up 23% year-on-year ([NPCI data via Jagran Josh](https://www.jagranjosh.com/general-knowledge/upi-creates-history-in-october-2026-transactions-cross-3153-lakh-crore-for-the-first-time-1820013888-1)). Merchant payments were 63% of volume but under a third of value — the asymmetry that makes the MDR fight consequential. The competitive map also moved: PhonePe led with 11.47 billion transactions (₹14.93 lakh crore) and Google Pay followed at 8.12 billion (₹10.53 lakh crore), while Navi edged past CRED into fourth place by value for the first time in five years — ₹62,492 crore against ₹62,102 crore ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/navi-dislodges-cred-from-fourth-spot-in-upi-rankings-by-value-after-five-years-article-14084607.html)).

## 4. DPDP's first gear engages — and collides with TRAI

The Consent Manager framework under Rule 4 of the DPDP Rules **commenced on 13 November** — the privacy regime's first operational phase, ahead of core notice-and-consent duties that arrive 13 May 2027. Legal analysis published this week flags the resulting dual-consent squeeze: TRAI's Third Amendment Regulations to TCCCPR expressly state that telecom-side consent compliance does **not** exempt senders from DPDP obligations, so promotional SMS and calls now need clean consent under both regimes simultaneously ([Elets eGov](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2026/11/trai-rules-and-dpdp-act-set-dual-consent-bars-for-marketing); [Legal 500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/privacy/marketing-consent-in-india-navigating-trais-tcccpr-and-the-dpdp-act)). Separately on the consumer-safety front, the home ministry's I4C warned (advisory dated 18 November, widely reported 23 November) that forgotten active WhatsApp Web sessions can be hijacked for impersonation and fraud ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/11/mha-warns-whatsapp-web-users-of-account-hijacking-advises-logging-out-after-every-session)) — relevant because WhatsApp remains a primary channel for payment scams.

## 5. The commerce layer keeps courting tribal India

TRIFED managing director M. Rajamurugan met ONDC chief operating officer Rohit Lohia at TRIFED headquarters on 18 November to explore bringing tribal artisans and Van Dhan Vikas Kendras onto the open network, aiming to widen market linkages and income for tribal producers ([Tribes India](https://www.facebook.com/tribesindia/posts/expandingdigitalmarketaccess-trifed-explores-furthering-e-commerce-opportunities/1460470149594477)). It rides shotgun with last week's GeM–TICCI MoU: two market rails, the same under-served constituency, in the same fortnight.

## Also noted

- **UIDAI keeps the identity layer moving**: [PIB reiterated on 20 November](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2339117&reg=17&lang=1) that mandatory biometric updates for children aged 5–17 remain free until 30 September 2027; UIDAI is also running Aadhaar enrolment and update counters at IITF 2026 (Bharat Mandapam) until 27 November, and a new Aadhaar Seva Kendra has opened in Leh (L1).
- **Watch**: the one missing date in the payments calendar is the MDR implementation itself — and account aggregator interoperability lands next Monday, 1 December.
