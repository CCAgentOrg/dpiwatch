# DPI Brief — September 13, 2026

With [Global Fintech Fest 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?id=151001&NoteId=151001&ModuleId=16&reg=3&lang=1) concluded in Mumbai and the [BRICS Summit](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/brics-new-delhi-declaration-building-for-resilience-innovation-cooperation-and-sustainability-september-12-2026) wrapping up in New Delhi, the last 24 hours delivered a dense cluster of announcements spanning identity, payments, agriculture and trust layers of India's DPI stack. Yesterday's brief covered UPI Tap & Pay, MyUPI and the BRICS CBDC push; today's edition tracks the developer tooling, ambient-payments and compliance stories still unfolding.

## 1. UIDAI opens up Aadhaar Face Authentication with a developer SDK and sandbox [L1 — Identity]

At GFF 2026, UIDAI launched a ["Face Auth SDK & Sandbox"](https://inc42.com/buzz/uidai-launches-sdk-sandbox-to-ease-aadhaar-face-authentication) — a plug-and-play kit plus a controlled testing environment for integrating Aadhaar face authentication into apps. The SDK ships with AI/ML-based liveness and anti-spoofing, encrypted auth-data handling, and targets BFSI players who must meet [RBI-mandated multi-factor authentication](https://www.technologyforyou.org/uidai-launches-aadhaar-face-authentication-sdk-and-sandbox-at-global-fintech-fest-2026). UIDAI chairman Neelkanth Mishra [told the fest](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/uidai-using-ai-extensively-to-detect-fraud-chairman-neelkanth-mishra/articleshow/133975532.cms) the authority is deploying AI extensively for fraud detection and expects agentic use cases to grow sharply over the next three years. In parallel, UIDAI [signed with MapmyIndia](https://newsonair.gov.in/uidai-signs-agreement-with-mapmyindia-to-enable-aadhaar-centres-on-mappls-app) to surface authorised enrolment centres on the Mappls app — a small but citizen-useful discovery fix.

## 2. BRICS New Delhi Declaration puts DPI on the multilateral agenda [L5 — Sectoral / Global DPI]

The [New Delhi Declaration](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/brics-new-delhi-declaration-building-for-resilience-innovation-cooperation-and-sustainability-september-12-2026) adopted at the 18th BRICS Summit notes the meeting of the Focus Group on Digital Public Infrastructure, welcomes the Digital BRICS Forum 2026, and endorses a **BRICS Network on Digital Agriculture** spanning AI, geospatial intelligence, IoT-enabled monitoring and advanced data analytics. Leaders also backed four farmer-centric initiatives — [BRICS AGRIN](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/brics-summit-2026-new-delhi-declaration-agriculture-agrin-wto-10875553/), a Global Forum on Farmers' Rights in Seed Systems, a centres-of-excellence network on agroecology, and the digital agriculture network — plus further work on a [Russia-backed BRICS Grain Exchange](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/agriculture/brics-delhi-declaration-grain-exchange-agrin-digital-agriculture-126091200679_1.html). For India's DPI diplomacy, this is the template for UPI-and-Aadhaar-style infrastructure going multilateral: non-binding, consensus-based, and increasingly formalised — with AgriStack-adjacent data-exchange norms likely to follow.

## 3. UPI moves into the car: ToneTag's DrivePay reaches IndianOil pumps [L2 — Payments]

ToneTag, Tata Motors and IndianOil launched [DrivePay](https://thisweekindia.news/tonetag-partners-with-tata-motors-indianoil-and-npci-to-launch-drivepay-in-car-fuel-payments) at GFF 2026 — India's first in-car fuel payment, built on NPCI's UPI Circle framework for ["Introduction of IoT Devices and Software on the UPI Ecosystem"](https://www.motoroids.com/news/tonetag-launches-in-car-fuel-payments-for-tata-cars). Connected-car owners at IndianOil outlets can pay for fuel from the dashboard without handing over a phone or card. It is the latest sign of payments migrating off the phone screen onto ambient surfaces — wearables, cars, appliances. That is genuinely convenient, but each new surface widens the attack plane: transaction limits, device-binding rules and dispute paths designed for phones will need rethinking when the phone is no longer in the loop.

## 4. Wizz Financial wires UPI into a global remittance wallet [L2 — Cross-border]

Wizz Financial launched a [unified cross-border app with its SWITCH interoperability engine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/wizz-financial-launches-cross-border-app-connecting-upi-to-global-markets/article71455858.ece), connecting its US and UAE propositions with Indian UPI accounts for [real-time inward transfers](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/story/wizz-financial-brings-upi-to-international-visitors-through-new-cross-border-wallet-554843-2026-09-11), payouts to 80+ countries, and UPI One World access for international visitors to India. The company bills it as the first wallet "where UPI meets remittance for every nationality." Read it carefully: this is private infrastructure layered on public rails — a usefully competitive remittance corridor for the diaspora, but "global UPI ecosystem" is the company's framing, not an NPCI interoperability milestone.

## 5. SBI targets DPDP compliance by December — eight months ahead of deadline [L7 — Trust]

India's largest lender has [procured the software and hardware](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/sbi-aims-for-dpdp-compliance-by-december-ahead-of-may-2027-deadline-126091101211_1.html) needed for Digital Personal Data Protection Act compliance and plans to be fully compliant by end-December 2026 — well ahead of the May 13, 2027 date when core obligations kick in (consent-manager Rule 4 arrives [November 13, 2026](https://www.siliconindia.com/article/beyond-privacy-policies-are-indian-enterprises-actually-ready-for-dpdp-compliance-nid-242231-cid-106.html)). The catch: the Data Protection Board that will hear complaints and levy penalties [still has no chairperson or members](https://www.livelaw.in/lawschool/articles/compliance-without-court-what-dpdpa-board-vacancy-means-may2027-549463). Institutions getting ready while the referee's bench sits empty is progress — but enforcement readiness is the part consumers will actually feel.

- **Biometric UPI crossed 6.29 billion transactions** as of August 31, per [NPCI figures announced at GFF](https://indiantelevision.com/mam/npci-unveils-upi-tap-pay-and-ai-powered-myupi-at-gff-2026) — device-native fingerprint and face authentication is now mainstream on the rails.
- **UIDAI has saturated 1.03 lakh schools** with [1.2 crore student biometric updates](https://newsonair.gov.in/uidai-completes-biometric-updates-for-1-2-crore-students-across-1-03-lakh-schools), keeping children's Aadhaar records current through the school system.
