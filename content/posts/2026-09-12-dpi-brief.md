# DPI Brief — September 12, 2026

With the [Global Fintech Fest 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?id=151001&NoteId=151001&ModuleId=16&reg=3&lang=1) wrapping up in Mumbai on September 11 and the [BRICS Summit](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/india-push-brics-digital-currency-link-despite-hurdles-sources-say-2026-09-10) opening in New Delhi today, the last 24 hours delivered a dense cluster of announcements spanning payments, identity, commerce and trust layers of India's DPI stack.

## 1. RBI launches UPI Tap & Pay and MyUPI, powered by the FiMI language model [L2 — Payments]

RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra unveiled two flagship UPI upgrades on the closing day of GFF 2026. [UPI Tap & Pay](https://www.rediff.com/business/report/rbi-introduces-upi-tap-to-pay-multi-currency-forex-on-bharat-connect-for-enhanced-digital-payments/20260911.htm) lets users tap an NFC-enabled smartphone on a compatible PoS terminal to pay **without internet on the phone**, with PIN-less transactions up to ₹5,000 (above which a UPI PIN is entered at the terminal). It works for both standard UPI and RuPay credit cards on UPI — a meaningful boost for offline and feature-lagging scenarios. Alongside it, [MyUPI](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-agentic-ai-products-fintechs-gff-2026) is built on NPCI's payments-focused small language model **FiMI** and gives users a consolidated dashboard of UPI transactions and AutoPay mandates across all their banks and apps. Its "User Controls" include a **Safety Switch** to decline suspicious debit mandates and the ability to delink a mobile number from UPI payments. In parallel, Bharat Connect's forex module expanded to [five additional currencies](https://www.rediff.com/business/report/rbi-introduces-upi-tap-to-pay-multi-currency-forex-on-bharat-connect-for-enhanced-digital-payments/20260911.htm) — Euro, British Pound, Canadian Dollar, Swiss Franc and UAE Dirham — integrating with Clearcorp's FX-Retail platform.

## 2. PM Modi sets a four-point trust agenda, proposes a Fintech Consumer Protection Index [L7 — Trust]

Inaugurating the fest, the Prime Minister [laid out four collective tasks](https://ddindia.co.in/2026/09/pm-modi-inaugurates-global-fintech-fest-2026-calls-for-next-generation-innovation-and-stronger-consumer-protection) for the sector: world-class cybersecurity, industry-owned **ethical data-protection standards**, a strong fintech-regulator innovation ecosystem, and a **Fintech Consumer Protection Index** on which every company can be transparently rated. He also pushed fintechs to extend into pensions and insurance for gig and blue-collar workers, and framed agentic AI, tokenisation and quantum as the [next frontier](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/gff-2026-pm-modi-asks-fintech-industry-to-turn-agentic-ai-tokenisation-quantum-potential-into-real-world-impact/articleshow/133928319.cms). For a trust-layer audience, the Consumer Protection Index is the sharpest ask — a public, comparable rating regime for how platforms treat users, echoing consumer-protection advocacy that has long demanded visible accountability beyond compliance filings.

## 3. UIDAI begins work to take Aadhaar global for NRIs and OCIs [L1 — Identity]

Sixteen years after the first Aadhaar was issued, UIDAI CEO Saurabh Vijay said at GFF 2026 that [work is underway to take Aadhaar international](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/in-2010-india-launched-aadhaar-card-16-years-later-uidai-says-work-underway-to-take-aadhaar-global-to-benefit-nris-and-ocis/articleshow/134061418.cms), aiming to build it into "best-in-class digital identity infrastructure" that can benefit NRIs and OCIs abroad. The statement follows UIDAI Chairman Neelkantha Mishra's earlier GFF remarks that authentication **outside India** is under active examination, alongside a 10-year roadmap covering AI-based fraud detection and post-quantum cryptography. If executed, cross-border Aadhaar authentication would mark the identity layer's biggest architectural expansion since its launch — and raise the familiar questions about data protection in foreign jurisdictions that the DPDP framework will have to answer.

## 4. India tables BRICS CBDC interlinking at the New Delhi summit [L2 — Cross-border payments]

As BRICS leaders gather in New Delhi on September 12–13 with India as chair, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/india-push-brics-digital-currency-link-despite-hurdles-sources-say-2026-09-10) India is pushing a proposal to **link member nations' central bank digital currencies** for cross-border payments — an idea the RBI first floated in January, building on the 2025 Rio declaration's call for payment-system interoperability. Crucially, Indian officials [frame the effort as efficiency, not de-dollarisation](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/india-brics-summit-2026-new-delhi-common-currency-cbdc-digital-rupee-cross-border-payments-rbi-narendra-modi/articleshow/134043376.cms): New Delhi is not backing a common BRICS currency or a SWIFT alternative, only faster, cheaper settlement between national systems. Geopolitical friction among members and slow global CBDC adoption remain the stated hurdles.

## 5. Visa and ONDC explore collaboration on tourism discovery [L4 — Commerce]

On the fest's final day, Visa and ONDC announced their [intent to explore a collaboration](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/services/travel/gff-2026-visa-ondc-explore-collaboration-to-simplify-tourism-discovery-in-india/articleshow/134059088.cms) to make India's tourism offerings easier to discover, combining Visa's digital commerce capabilities with ONDC's open-network infrastructure to connect travellers with destinations, itineraries and local businesses. The deal targets ONDC's [demand-side gap in tourism](https://www.passionateinmarketing.com/visa-and-ondc-announce-intent-to-explore-collaboration-to-enhance-tourism-discovery-in-india) — a sector where the network's supply side has grown faster than consumer pull, and where global card-network distribution could seed first-time network usage among inbound travellers.

## Quick hits

- **NPCI's FY26 books:** standalone net surplus fell ~12% to ₹1,362 crore despite ~21% revenue growth to ₹4,240 crore, as [marketing and AI costs surged](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/business/npci-net-surplus-falls-12-to-1362-crore-in-fy26-despite-21-revenue-growth) — an investment-phase signal for the operator of UPI, which now [handles ~89% of India's digital transactions](https://www.niftytrader.in/markets/why-npcis-fy26-profit-fell-12-despite).
- **TRAI's 5G slicing framework meets resistance:** Jio, Airtel and Vodafone Idea have [pushed back against proposed QoS rules](https://www.lightreading.com/5g/indian-telcos-push-back-against-proposed-5g-slicing-rules) for network slicing, arguing capacity mandates would restrict flexibility — relevant to how differentiated network services get regulated as DPI delivery moves onto private 5G.
- **Digital-first agro-chemical licensing:** the Insecticides (Amendment) Rules 2026 take effect [September 15](https://www.global-agriculture.com/crop-protection/indias-digital-first-insecticide-licensing-rules-take-effect-september-15/), moving licensing and record-keeping for ~300,000 pesticide sale points fully online — a quiet AgriStack-adjacent digitisation mandate with a compliance deadline next week.
