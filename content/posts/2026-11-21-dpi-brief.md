# DPI Brief — November 21, 2026

The trust layer owned Friday: CERT-In closed the week with a critical advisory confirming payment fraud has jumped from banks to the tech service providers that run government-linked payment workflows, while separately flagging AI-driven offensive frameworks in the wild. A regulator is weighing its own AI model as DPI, India's biggest state exam commission is making DigiLocker the default verification rail, and ABHA crossed another milestone.

## L7 · Trust: CERT-In's critical advisory — payment workflows are the new attack surface

In advisory [CIAD-2026-0062](https://www.cert-in.org.in/s2cMainServlet?pageid=PUBVLNOTES02&VLCODE=CIAD-2026-0062), issued November 20 at Critical severity, CERT-In reported unauthorised financial transactions "not limited to BFSI entities" — they now reach technology service providers supporting government-linked payment processes and platforms handling vendor and partner payouts. The modus operandi is workflow abuse, not API hacking: [attackers alter business records or approval relationships](https://www.financialexpress.com/life/technology/ai-driven-tools-making-cyberattacks-faster-cert-in-issues-critical-alert/4390025) and then push transactions through systems that remain perfectly legitimate. [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/india-cyber-agency-reports-unauthorised-transactions-payment-firms-2026-11-20) neither the amounts nor the affected entities were disclosed.

In separate threat-hunting, CERT-In identified AI-driven agentic offensive frameworks (AOFs) — automated reconnaissance and attack chains — across sectors, and pointed organisations to its earlier advisories (CIAD-2026-0046, 0049, 0058) and the [CISG-2026-02 blueprint](https://www.cnbctv18.com/technology/cert-in-warns-of-unauthorised-payment-transactions-ai-driven-cyberattacks-across-sectors-20038937.htm) on defending against AI-assisted exploitation. It follows a spate of attacks on banks and non-bank lenders; earlier this month CERT-In named the group behind a wave of AI-driven attacks on financial firms, with at least two tools [linked to China](https://www.telegraphindia.com/india/cert-in-flags-unauthorised-transactions-at-banking-service-providers-warns-of-ai-driven-cyberattacks/cid/2190485). For DPI watchers the significance is blunt: DBT disbursement chains depend on exactly these service providers, and the trust layer's own agency is now documenting compromises in that layer.

## L3 · Documents: UPPSC makes DigiLocker the default

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission announced on November 20 that it will move to [100 per cent digital applications with DigiLocker-based document verification within six months](https://www.jagranjosh.com/news/uppsc-to-verify-documents-via-digilocker-only-digital-applications-allowed-after-six-months-189140), ending physical certificate submissions — chairman Sanjay Shrinet framing it ahead of the end of his tenure. [Hindustan Times confirms](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/lucknow-news/uppsc-to-go-fully-online-for-mains-applications-document-verification-101795113132010.html) mains applications and verification both go fully online. This is the documents layer doing what it was built for — but at scale it is also a stress test: every certificate an UPPSC candidate holds must actually exist as a DigiLocker-issued document, and slow-to-digitise issuers become the bottleneck lakhs of applicants feel.

## L6/L7 · SEBI weighs its own AI model "as part of our DPI"

At the CII Financing Summit on Friday, SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey said the regulator is [examining whether to build its own AI model as part of India's Digital Public Infrastructure](https://www.aninews.in/news/business/sebi-examining-feasibility-of-own-ai-model-under-digital-public-infrastructure-chairman20261120124602) — "there are a lot of risks in AI, and opportunities too. Guardrails are also needed." A market regulator treating an AI model as DPI is a notable expansion of what the term covers. The same appearance carried an identity-layer datapoint: NRIs can now complete KYC through video verification, [with the India-travel requirement removed](https://www.aninews.in/news/business/sebi-examining-feasibility-of-own-ai-model-under-digital-public-infrastructure-chairman20261120124602) — video KYC quietly becoming the default for a global user base.

## L5 · Sectoral: ABHA nears 99 crore; AgriStack's consent clock is running

The [ABDM dashboard](https://dashboard.abdm.gov.in/abdm) shows 98.99 crore ABHA accounts as of November 19 — 2.71 lakh created that day, 45.26 lakh this month — putting the health ID layer within reach of the 100-crore mark. On the agriculture side, the [AgriStack Farmer ID count stood at 11 crore](https://agrospectrumindia.com/features/67/35171/agristack-ten-questions-before-you-plug-in.html) as of late October, and the DPDP compliance clock is now live: registered consent managers have been permitted since November 13, 2026, with full obligations biting from May 13, 2027. Two sectoral stacks, two maturities: one sprinting toward a population-scale round number, the other discovering that consent architecture is the hard part.

## L6 · Governance: CPGRAMS closure quality under dispute

Farmer and rural-worker organisations backing the Samyukta Kisan Morcha's November 26 protest call claim [lakhs of MGNREGA-related grievances filed on CPGRAMS in September–October from six states were "falsely closed without any action"](https://countercurrents.org/2026/11/rural-workers-stand-with-skms-call-for-nationwide-protest-on-november-26) — an activist claim, not an official finding, but one aimed directly at the gap between disposal volume and disposal quality. It lands just as the accounting establishment was ordered to watch the portal daily — the CGA's November 18 monitoring mandate, with DARPG reporting daily disposals above 8,600 in early November. When the metric is closures per day, "closed" becomes the easiest output to manufacture.

## Layer watch

- **L1 · Identity:** Exam bodies keep pulling Aadhaar into registration plumbing — SSC's November 17 advisory makes biometric unlocking a standard step for candidates, and UIDAI's free email-update offer via the Aadhaar app runs until December 31, 2026. Courts, meanwhile, continue to fence Aadhaar's DOB claims (see yesterday's Delhi HC note).
- **L2 · Payments:** The merchant fee remains dateless, and the industry is getting louder about it: the Payments Council of India is [pressing the finance ministry and NPCI for a firm MDR timeline](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/payment-firms-seek-clarity-on-mdr-rollout-for-select-upi-transactions/articleshow/135939899.cms), flagging that government incentives for UPI and RuPay debit processing have gone unpaid for over 18 months, and that the MDR-funded Vyapaar Saksham small-merchant scheme is still waiting on the same trigger. MeitY's Jitendra Singh separately flagged [digital payments as a cooperation area with Egypt](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2026/11/19/india-s-digital-payments-ecosystem-important-area-for-cooperation-with-egypt-jitendra-singh.amp.html).
- **L4 · Commerce:** GeM's Samvad 2026 event outlined five seller-facing reforms — transaction charges cut by roughly [33–96 per cent depending on category and order value](https://gemtenderservice.com/gem-portal-updates-2026), cheaper OEM assessment, TReDS integration for early invoice payment, the SAHAY working-capital product, and a Trusted Seller ecosystem.

**Watch item:** how quickly the CIAD-2026-0062 advisory translates into audited controls across government-linked payment service providers — and, as ever, December 9 (Supreme Court DPDP hearing) and the still-missing MDR date.
