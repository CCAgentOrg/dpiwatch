# DPI Brief — October 31, 2026

**Top DPI headlines for October 31, 2026** — TRAI drafts the telecom sector's AI rulebook, UPI starts booking the Burj Khalifa, ONDC's DigiDukaan pulls the FMCG majors into a room, and three new Aadhaar Seva Kendras open in Kerala while 73,000-odd children there wait on biometric updates.

## [L7 · Trust] TRAI proposes the rulebook for AI on telecom networks

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India has [released draft guidelines](https://trai.gov.in/notifications/press-release/trai-releases-draft-guidelines-responsible-use-artificial-intelligence) on the responsible use of AI in telecommunication networks and services, open for stakeholder comments until November 27, 2026. The draft is meant to implement the [India AI Governance Guidelines](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/technology/trai-ai-guidelines-indian-telecom-sector-ai-governance-telecom-regulations-enn26103005933) (issued February 2026) at sector level. As [reported by PTI](https://www.rediff.com/business/report/trai-proposes-ai-policies-for-telecom-networks/20261030.htm), it would require service providers to adopt board-approved AI policies, classify AI systems into high-, medium- and low-risk categories, maintain a central incident database, and report critical and high-severity AI incidents to TRAI in a prescribed format — all overseen by an AI Governance and Safety Committee with board approval. This matters beyond telecom: UPI transactions, Aadhaar authentications and DigiLocker fetches all ride telco networks, making this the first sector-specific codification of India's national AI governance framework on the infrastructure the rest of the DPI stack stands on.

## [L2 · Payments] UPI now books the Burj Khalifa and Universal Studios Singapore

The Finance Ministry [announced on October 30](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/upi-now-accepted-for-online-bookings-at-burj-khalifa-universal-studios-singapore-and-other-major-attractions-564852-2026-10-30) that UPI is now accepted for eligible online bookings at major international attractions — the Burj Khalifa in Dubai and Resorts World Sentosa properties in Singapore including Universal Studios Singapore, the Singapore Oceanarium and Adventure Cove Waterpark. The expansion rides [NPCI International Payments Limited partnerships](https://www.rediff.com/business/report/upi-payments-for-burj-khalifa-singapore-attractions/20261030.htm) with NEOPAY in the UAE and FOMO Pay in Singapore. Uzbekistan is lined up to become the eleventh country accepting UPI, off a commercial agreement announced August 30 during the Prime Minister's visit. The shift to notice: UPI acceptance abroad has so far meant street vendors and QR codes; moving into online travel booking attacks the last place Indian tourists reliably fall back to international cards.

## [L4 · Commerce] ONDC's DigiDukaan gets the FMCG majors in one room

At a DPIIT roundtable on trade digitisation on October 30, Secretary Amardeep Singh Bhatia said [open digital infrastructure is critical](https://money.rediff.com/news/market/open-digital-infra-key-to-india-retail-digitalization-dpiit/57212720261030) to digitising India's kirana retail, with DPIIT and ONDC building a connected general-trade ecosystem through [ONDC's DigiDukaan](https://www.thehawk.in/news/india/ondc-digidukaan-to-empower-kirana-retailers-via-digital-procurement-supply-chain-efficiency-goyal). DigiDukaan digitises kiranas as *buyers*: one interface to discover and order across multiple brands, distributors and wholesalers, with visibility into pricing, trade schemes and stock. As of September it has connected over 21,000 retailers with more than 450 brands across Hyderabad, Jaipur and Mumbai — and the roundtable drew senior representatives from HUL, Coca-Cola India, ITC, P&G, Marico, Britannia, Adani Wilmar and Haldiram's. Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal's framing: "when open digital infrastructure is built around the needs of the small retailer, adoption follows." DigiDukaan is ONDC's B2B procurement play — demand-side digitisation of the kirana network rather than another consumer shopping app.

## [L1 · Identity] Kerala adds three Aadhaar Seva Kendras — and exposes the biometric update cliff

UIDAI opened an Aadhaar Seva Kendra in [Kannur on October 29](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/kerala/uidai-run-aadhaar-seva-kendra-opens-in-kannur/article71639692.ece), its eighth in Kerala, with Kozhikode following October 30 and Kasaragod on October 31 — enrolment, corrections, biometric updates and grievance redressal under one roof. The sharper story is in the numbers buried in the same report: 73,645 children in Kannur district alone are yet to complete mandatory Aadhaar biometric updates — 39,450 who have turned five and 34,195 who have turned fifteen. Miss the window and services stall: school and college admissions, scholarships, PAN, DigiLocker, NEET and KEAM registrations all sit downstream of a current biometric. Physical service-centre expansion is the unglamorous half of identity infrastructure; it is also the half that decides whether a 145-crore registry stays usable at the edges.
