# DPI Brief — November 12, 2026

**Top DPI headlines for November 12, 2026** — the UPI merchant charge is officially in limbo even as payment companies build for it, DPDP's consent-manager phase opens this week, TRAI prices IP interconnection for the first time while fining MTNL, and two identity stories show Aadhaar meeting its limits — in court and at the ration shop.

## [L2 · Payments] NPCI still won't say when UPI's merchant charge starts — fintechs are building for it anyway

Two days past Deepavali, NPCI has [still not announced a date](https://www.thehindu.com/business/Economy/two-days-past-deepavali-npci-yet-to-announce-a-date-for-upi-mdr-rollout/article71689098.ece) for the MDR rollout on UPI person-to-merchant transactions above ₹2,000 — The Hindu reports "there is no announcement on this at the moment," despite the October 13 deferment promise of implementation "immediately post Diwali." The industry, meanwhile, is pricing the charge as if it were a done deal: Pine Labs told analysts the 0.4% MDR could unlock **₹80–230 crore in annual revenue**, and is deploying roughly **10 lakh soundboxes** to capture merchant UPI flows it had been ceding to rivals ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-upi-mdr-annual-revenue-pine-labs)). AvenuesAI (Infibeam) — 22% of whose transactions ride on UPI — said the same on its Q2 FY27 call, launching consumer app RediffPay ahead of expected monetisation ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-avenuesai-profit-revenue-surges)). Payment companies are building revenue models on a notification that doesn't yet exist; the longer the silence, the more this looks like a rollout by press release.

## [L7 · Trust] DPDP phase two opens this week: consent managers get a registry, companies get a bill

The second implementation phase of the DPDP Act — registration of **consent managers**, the interoperable platforms through which citizens will give, view and withdraw consent — takes effect this week. The [Daily Pioneer](https://dailypioneer.com/amp/story/analysis/data-protection-in-india-the-compliance-gap) dates it **November 13**, twelve months into the 18-month phase-in that began with the November 2025 rules, with the remaining substantive obligations (privacy notices, breach reporting, security safeguards) landing May 13, 2027; one [ETCFO report](https://cfo.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/cfo-tech/dpdp-compliance-cfos-grapple-with-costs-consent-management-and-supply-chain-risks/135672678) dates the same milestone November 12 — even compliance trackers can't quite agree on the day. Undisputed is the cost: CFOs describe six-to-nine months onboarding consent-management software and security upgrades, with third-party supply-chain risk the hardest part. The Pioneer piece lands the sharpest counterpoint: breaches at State Bank of India (October 2026) and Bank of Baroda (July 2026) exposed lakhs of customers' data before detailed obligations became enforceable. A consent registry built before the enforcement date is infrastructure ahead of the law — the question is whether May 2027 arrives with a Board that can use it.

## [L7 · Trust] TRAI's interconnection reset: IP ports get ceilings, MTNL gets fined

TRAI released the Telecommunication Interconnection (Port Charges) (Third Amendment) Regulations on November 9, bringing **IP-based interconnection ports under regulation for the first time** — annual ceilings of **₹5.6 lakh per 1 Gbps port and ₹42.3 lakh per 10 Gbps port**, effective January 1, 2027, supplementing the legacy E1/TDM framework; operators may negotiate below the cap ([Economic Times/PTI](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/telecom/telecom-news/trai-sets-port-charge-ceilings-for-ip-based-interconnection-effective-jan-2027/articleshow/135683265.cms)). In the same week, the regulator imposed a **₹6 lakh financial disincentive on MTNL** for wireline broadband quality-of-service breaches in the quarter ending June 2026 — an order dated November 10, disclosed to the exchanges on November 11 ([ScanX](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/mahanagar-telephone-nigam-fined-6-lakh-trai-qos-breaches/55923589), [Kalkine](https://kalkinemedia.com/in/news/announcements/trai-imposes-rs-6-lakh-penalty-on-mtnl-nsemtnl-for-broadband-quality-service-breach)). A ₹6 lakh fine against a state-owned telco is small money; that it was disclosed at all is the part worth watching.

## [L1 × L5 · Identity] Aadhaar meets its limits: a court says it can't prove age, Karnataka pushes its e-KYC deadline again

The Madhya Pradesh High Court has held that **an Aadhaar card is not conclusive proof of age** in motor accident compensation cases — Justice Amit Seth ruled the postmortem report's medical assessment has primacy, enhancing a deceased minor's compensation from ₹5.45 lakh to ₹8.25 lakh ([Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/legal-news/postmortem-report-aadhaar-proof-age-rules-madhya-pradesh-high-court-10958941), [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/madhya-pradesh/2026/Nov/11/mp-hc-says-aadhaar-card-not-conclusive-proof-of-age-post-mortem-report-more-reliable)). A useful statement of what Aadhaar is: an identity token, not a verified fact-register — the gap between the two is where compensation claims get fought. Meanwhile at the PDS edge, Karnataka has extended the mandatory **e-KYC deadline for APL and BPL ration cardholders to November 30** — the latest in a series of slips, after server failures at fair-price shops disrupted verification ([Daijiworld](https://www.daijiworld.com/news/newsDisplay?newsID=1334970)). Deadlines that keep moving usually mean the mandate outran the plumbing — biometric devices, connectivity, operator training at the last mile.

## [L4 × L5 · Commerce] Network scale-up day: DigiDukaan's 21,000 retailers and AgriStack's "UPI moment"

November 11 was a good day for official scale-up narratives. At a Chintan Shivir on trade digitisation chaired by Piyush Goyal, ONDC's **DigiDukaan** reported over **21,000 neighbourhood retailers connected with more than 450 brands** across Hyderabad, Jaipur and Mumbai as of September 2026 — up from 12,000-odd previously ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetail.aspx?PRID=2330634&reg=+3&lang=1), [DD News](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/digidukaan-connects-over-21000-retailers-with-more-than-450-brands-across-three-cities-piyush-goyal-chairs-chintan-shivir-on-trade-digitisation)). The same day, at the Kisan Tak Bharat Agriculture Conference, AgriStack's Devesh Chaturvedi described an **"UPI-like revolution"** arriving in farming via AI on the agri-stack ([India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/ai-powered-agristack-akin-to-upi-revolution-in-india-experts-say-3036840-2026-11-11)). Both are milestones dressed as revolutions: DigiDukaan's figures carry a September 2026 cutoff, and AgriStack's UPI comparison invites the scrutiny UPI earned only after a decade of transaction-level transparency.
