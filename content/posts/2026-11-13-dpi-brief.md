# DPI Brief — November 13, 2026

Two infrastructure stories dominated the last 24 hours — NPCI quietly wiring up the funding plumbing for the UPI MDR era, and the eMudhra certificate crisis reaching the point where Windows itself will stop trusting the CA next week. Around them, Aadhaar's paperless verification network keeps compounding, and AgriStack farmer IDs crossed from registry statistics into mandatory welfare paperwork.

## 1. NPCI notifies UPI Vyapaar Saksham — the MDR rollout's acceptance-infrastructure leg [L2: Payments]

In a [circular dated November 11](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/finance/npci-notifies-upi-vyapaar-saksham-scheme-to-push-small-merchant-acceptance-to-be-funded-from-upi-mdr-20032322.htm), NPCI introduced the **UPI Vyapaar Saksham Scheme**, a three-year incentive programme to push QR codes and soundboxes into small merchants — and its funding model confirms the merchant-discount-rate era is imminent. Every issuing bank, acquiring bank and payer PSP will contribute **5% of its UPI MDR share** to a dedicated fund, and the scheme runs for three years *from the date MDR on UPI is implemented*. That date still isn't announced — NPCI [deferred the October 15 start](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/economy/story/upi-payments-above-rs2000-to-attract-04-mdr-after-diwali-who-pays-who-is-exempt-and-when-charges-begin-566737-2026-11-10) and told the Supreme Court on November 3 that the fee is needed to sustain the ecosystem and cannot be passed to customers — but you don't build a MDR-funded scheme unless the MDR is coming.

The fund splits three ways: 30% for new-merchant onboarding ([₹300 per physical QR code, ₹750 per soundbox](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/npci-introduces-upi-vyapaar-saksham-scheme-to-boost-digital-payments-among-small-merchants-offers-incentives-for-qr-codes-and-soundboxes/135771249), gated by the merchant doing at least 25 eligible transactions in two months of a quarter and ₹5,000 monthly value); 50% for transaction growth (0.03% of eligible value, requiring 5% quarter-on-quarter growth, with a 50% bonus in target geographies); and 20% for incremental volumes in targeted regions. Eligible transactions are settled UPI payments up to ₹2,000 at small merchants with turnover up to ₹40 lakh, focused on Tier 3–6 centres, the North-East, J&K and Ladakh. NPCI is explicitly [banning split transactions](https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/npci-introduces-upi-vyapaar-saksham-scheme-to-boost-qr-code-soundbox-adoption-among-small-merchants-633372) engineered to duck the ₹2,000 threshold, and double-dipping with NABARD-style subsidy schemes. Watch: this scheme's start date is the public's best signal of when MDR goes live.

## 2. eMudhra crisis widens: 16 compromised certificates, Windows pulls trust on November 16 [L7: Trust]

The [eMudhra/emSign CA compromise](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/emudhra-fiasco-widens-eight-more-cas-revoked-microsoft-pulls-trust-630303) keeps getting worse. The company revoked **eight more RSA intermediate CA certificates on November 7**, bringing the total to 16 — double the eight ECDSA intermediates whose private keys were trivially recoverable when the incident broke in October. The root cause is embarrassing: a homegrown Python script written to move keys between hardware security modules truncated ECDSA private keys to a single byte (one key was literally the integer 4), and re-imported RSA keys from ordinary process memory, violating the CA's own CPS requirements. An earlier warning sign in June 2025 went unaddressed, and eMudhra admits it never verified key strength or that certificate keys matched their HSM originals.

The consequences land next week: **Microsoft removes the remaining 10 eMudhra roots from Windows, Edge and Azure on November 16**, Apple follows on November 30, and eMudhra exits the public TLS business entirely, with KPMG's independent review due December 4. For consumers, the practical takeaway: if a government, bank or utility site you use still chains to eMudhra, the operator has days to migrate. We continue tracking this incident at [CyberOpsec's India PKI Watch](https://cashlessconsumer.github.io/india-pki-watch/).

## 3. Aadhaar App: 300 organisations now do paperless offline verification [L1: Identity]

UIDAI has onboarded **300 Offline Verification Seeking Entities** since the Aadhaar App launched in January — triple the 100 entities reported in April — spanning [hotels, healthcare, education, events and the gig economy](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/wealth/save/offline-aadhaar-verification-to-get-easier-for-users-as-uidai-onboards-300-entities-to-complete-process/articleshow/135607596.cms). The mechanism is the privacy-respecting kind: consent-based, selective disclosure, no Aadhaar number shared with the verifier and no connection to UIDAI's central database — a direct attack on the (illegal but rampant) practice of collecting photocopies. The app itself is near [7.5 crore downloads](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/uidai-onboards-300-entities-for-aadhaar-offline-verification-app-downloads-near-7-5-crore), with 86.37 lakh mobile-number updates, 22.16 lakh address updates and 40.25 lakh email updates done without an Aadhaar-centre visit. The compounding curve here — 100 to 300 entities in seven months — is worth watching more than the absolute number.

## 4. AgriStack farmer IDs become mandatory welfare rails — with visible friction [L5: Sectoral]

The 11-crore Farmer ID milestone (hit [October 24, five months ahead of target](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/agri-business/centre-issues-11-crore-farmer-ids-under-agristack-26-are-women/article71620251.ece), 26% women) is now translating into hard requirements: Odisha has made the AgriStack Farmer ID plus Aadhaar [mandatory documents for Rabi 2026-27 crop insurance enrolment](https://pragativadi.com/pmfby-rabi-2026-27-odisha-free-crop-insurance-registration-till-dec-15) with a December 15 deadline, and Andaman & Nicobar farmers face a November 30 cutoff to keep PM-KISAN and subsidy access. Officials are billing AgriStack as ["the next UPI"](https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/ai-powered-agristack-akin-to-upi-revolution-in-india-experts-say-3036840-2026-11-11). But the friction is real: Maharashtra was forced to allow **offline crop registration for ~7 million farmers** because the Centre's Digital Crop Survey app kept glitching. DPI that gates welfare is only as good as its uptime — the offline fallback is the correct consumer-protective design, and other states should pre-commit to it rather than improvise.

## 5. One year of DPDP Rules — six months to the compliance cliff [L7: Trust]

Today marks one year since the [DPDP Rules were notified on November 13, 2025](https://www.barandbench.com/Law-School/iala-launches-batch-2-of-its-online-certificate-course-on-data-privacy-law-dpdp-implementation-ai-register-now). Every organisation has until **May 13, 2027** to be compliant, with penalties up to ₹250 crore per breach category. The transition clock is now past two-thirds; expect consent-manager onboarding, breach-intimation drill and DPIA churn to dominate the trust layer's news cycle into the new year.

**Quiet layers:** DigiLocker (L3) and CPGRAMS/eOffice (L6) saw no major national announcements in the window — routine issuance continued, including NPS pension documents now accessible via DigiLocker. On the payments data front, UPI logged [916.9 million transactions on Diwali (Nov 8), up 32% year-on-year](https://money.rediff.com/amp/news/market/upi-transactions-soar-32-on-diwali-to-916-9-million/57839920261110), and October closed at 25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore.
