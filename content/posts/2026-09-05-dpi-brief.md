# DPI Brief — September 05, 2026

**Three days before Global Fintech Fest opens in Mumbai, India's DPI stack is doing housekeeping in public:** TRAI rewriting how telecom complaints get resolved, GeM wiring circular-economy products into government buying, health and farm registries pivoting from construction to adoption — and an RTI showing the identity layer can't audit one of its own riskiest data flows.

## 1. TRAI drafts new telecom complaint rules while telcos fight its 5G slicing QoS plan (L7 Trust)

The trust layer ran on two fronts this week. TRAI released the [Draft Telecom Consumers Complaint Redressal (Fourth Amendment) Regulation, 2026](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/trai-releases-draft-rules-to-modernise-consumer-complaint-redressal-system) on Thursday, an overhaul of how operator-level complaints are logged, escalated and resolved — the redressal machinery most consumers meet only when something breaks. Simultaneously, the regulator's proposed quality-of-service norms for 5G network slicing hit a wall: Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel and Vodafone Idea [told TRAI the framework is technically impractical and anti-ease-of-doing-business](https://www.livemint.com/industry/telecom/reliance-jio-airtel-vodafone-idea-trai-5g-slicing-norms-service-quality-rules-telecom-companies-india-11788502641215.html), opposing resource-utilisation benchmarks in submissions dated September 2. Counter-comments are due September 7. The consumer stake: sliced 5G will sell premium speed tiers to some subscribers while sharing the same physical network — without ex-ante QoS rules, ordinary users absorb the degradation silently.

## 2. GeM goes circular and opens to the world: textiles MoU, ₹20 lakh crore milestone, global tenders (L4 Commerce)

Government e-Marketplace signed an MoU with the Textiles Committee on September 3 to [route recycled and upcycled textile products into public procurement](https://ddindia.co.in/2026/09/gem-textiles-committee-sign-mou-to-boost-procurement-of-recycled-upcycled-textiles) — creating certified GeM categories so textile waste, factory scrap and second-hand clothing become government-purchasable goods, with built-in demand for MSME recyclers. CEO Mihir Kumar separately confirmed GeM has [crossed ₹20 lakh crore in cumulative transaction value](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/gem-readies-global-tenders-as-ftas-open-government-procurement-to-foreign-suppliers-14020713.html) and is preparing to admit eligible foreign suppliers as FTA procurement commitments kick in. Two directions at once: greener domestic buying, and the first real contest for domestic sellers on their home turf.

## 3. ABDM's next phase is adoption, not accumulation — 93.95 crore ABHA IDs built, now make them used (L5 Sectoral)

Health Minister J.P. Nadda on Friday reviewed ABDM and declared it [one of the world's largest digital health ecosystems: over 93.95 crore ABHA IDs and 105 crore linked health records](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/ayushman-bharat-digital-mission-emerges-as-one-of-worlds-largest-digital-health-ecosystems-with-over-93-95-crore-abha-ids-j-p-nadda), with integration deepening into PM-JAY, CGHS, ESIC and Nikshay, plus Model Districts and Aarogya Setu 2.0. But his own emphasis — "the next phase must focus on maximizing adoption and utilization" — matches the ground truth: Haryana has onboarded 97.9% of targeted *government* facilities, yet officials admit [low private-sector participation could stall the mission](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/low-private-sector-adoption-could-slow-digital-health-mission-says-acs/articleshow/133745357.cms) in a state where private hospitals deliver most care. An ABHA that only public hospitals honor is a ID card, not infrastructure.

## 4. AgriStack incentives flow: Telangana nets another ₹175 crore as 1.93 crore farm fields get photographed (L5 Sectoral)

The Union Agriculture Ministry's August 31 letter sanctioned Telangana [an additional ₹175 crore SASCI incentive for completing the first milestone of its AgriStack-based fertiliser distribution system](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/telangana/centre-sanctions-one-more-incentive-of-175-cr-to-tg-for-agristack-implementation/article71411542.ece) — linking farmer records to fertiliser transactions for supply-chain tracking, and taking total central incentives to the state to roughly ₹599 crore. The state is meanwhile running one of India's largest digital crop surveys: [10,000 volunteers photographing 1.93 crore farm plots across 10,622 villages](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/193-cr-farm-fields-to-be-photographed-as-part-of-digital-agri-survey-1984377) at ₹7 per photo, with agriculture officers cross-checking and farmers getting an SMS correction loop. Notably, season-end procurement will be linked to crop registration — turning survey compliance into a de facto benefit gate.

## 5. Aadhaar's audit blind spot: RTI reveals UIDAI didn't track Bihar DOB changes after pension hike (L1 Identity)

An RTI response reported by Mid-day on September 3 shows UIDAI [kept no separate record of Aadhaar date-of-birth changes in Bihar](https://www.mid-day.com/news/india-news/article/bihar-pension-hike-uidai-had-no-separate-data-on-aadhaar-dob-changes-after-nitish-kumars-announcement-23648133) following the announcement that old-age, disability and widow pensions would jump from ₹400 to ₹1,100 — no month-wise or district-wise data, despite eligibility for the hike hinging on age. The identity layer cannot currently answer whether its own database was massaged around a benefit announcement. Service capacity is expanding in parallel: a new [Aadhaar Seva Kendra opened in Tiruvannamalai](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/collector-inaugurates-aadhaar-seva-kendra-in-tiruvannamalai/article71424190.ece), UIDAI's 13th in Tamil Nadu with 17 more planned. More service centres fix access; they don't fix auditability.

## Quick takes

- **L3 Documents:** IIT Madras has made [DigiLocker verification mandatory for all Indian GATE 2027 candidates](https://education.sakshi.com/en/gate/education-news/gate-2027-registration-big-change-introduced-digilocker-verification-now-mandatory-all-candidates-199104) — application details are fetched directly from DigiLocker, making the document locker a hard dependency for a national exam (applications close September 27).
- **L2 Payments:** NPCI is readying [UPI AutoPay interoperability](https://www.livemint.com/economy/npci-readies-upi-autopay-interoperability-for-consumers-and-merchants-11787898320112.html) — letting consumers port recurring mandates across UPI apps and merchants move them between payment gateways — expected to be announced at GFF. And after [Uzbekistan's go-live on August 30](https://www.ibtimes.co.in/upi-gambit-how-indias-payment-revolution-goes-global-what-comes-next-905362), UPI now runs in 11 countries.
- **L6 Governance:** CPGRAMS disposed [13,419 grievances in a single day](https://pgportal.gov.in/darpgdashboard) (September 2); DARPG reports ~38,000 grievances and 4,000 appeals cleared over August 24–27.

**Watch:** Global Fintech Fest, September 8–11, Mumbai. AutoPay portability, agent payments and the UPI global rollout are all queued for the main stage.
