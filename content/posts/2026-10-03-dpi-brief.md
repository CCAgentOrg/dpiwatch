# DPI Brief — October 03, 2026

Window covered: **8:00 a.m. IST on October 2 to 8:00 a.m. IST on October 3.** The last day brought fresh payment activity data, a merchant protest over an upcoming UPI fee, and a reported DigiLocker procurement process. No comparable, date-matched official launch surfaced across the other core DPI layers.

## 1. UPI’s first half grew 27%; September’s monthly dip needs context

A [PTI report published by *The Hindu* late on October 2](https://www.thehindu.com/business/upi-volume-jumps-27-to-touch-145-bn-in-first-half/article71538372.ece), citing data released by NPCI, puts UPI volume at about **145 billion transactions in April–September**, up 27% from 114 billion in the same period a year earlier. September alone recorded **24.07 billion** transactions, 1.7% below August’s 24.5 billion. But September had one fewer day: the report says daily transactions averaged **802 million**, against 791 million in August. The monthly total fell; average daily use did not. NPCI’s [UPI product-statistics page](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-statistics) is the primary series for tracking the next release.

That distinction matters just ahead of the new merchant discount rate (MDR). These figures describe adoption before the fee takes effect; they cannot tell us whether merchants will change acceptance, prices or checkout choices afterward. Treating one shorter month’s lower total as evidence that UPI use is retreating would overread the data.

## 2. “No UPI Day” was a merchant protest, not a network shutdown

Some retailers acted on October 2 against the forthcoming MDR: [Nashik traders were reported to have observed the protest](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/nashik/traders-across-nashik-observed-no-upi-day/articleshow/134644661.cms), while [Ahmedabad reporting described a mixed response](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/ahmedabad/mixed-response-to-no-upi-day-in-ahmedabad/amp_articleshow/134645458.cms). This was voluntary and uneven—not a nationwide UPI outage or an NPCI suspension. The [NPCI MDR FAQ](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/FA_Qs_Merchant_Discount_Rate_MDR_on_Select_UPI_P2_M_Transactions_58dba1d39e.pdf) sets out the scheduled October 15 start for specified high-value person-to-merchant transactions. The consumer-facing question is whether the charge stays with merchants, as the policy intends, or reappears indirectly in prices or reduced payment choice. The day’s action shows that merchant acceptance is now part of the policy debate, not just an implementation detail.

## 3. DigiLocker may add private integrators; selection is not final

An [*Economic Times* report on October 2](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/govt-close-to-picking-private-ai-integrator-for-digilocker/articleshow/134625439.cms), citing government officials, says MeitY is close to empanelling private AI integrators to find and onboard document requesters, connect them to DigiLocker and grow transaction volumes. It names CoRover and Kyndryl as firms that cleared initial rounds and were undergoing technical evaluation. That is a reported procurement status, not a published award: no winner should be treated as selected on this evidence.

The same report quotes an official saying integrators must not retain, cache, profile or run secondary analytics on document data accessed through DigiLocker under the DPDP regime. That safeguard is consequential precisely because a larger requester network creates more points where document access can be mishandled. The test is not only what the contract promises, but whether access is purpose-limited, auditable and technically prevented from turning into a copy-and-keep pipeline.

## Seven-layer scan

- **L1 Identity:** no new date-matched UIDAI or eKYC change surfaced.
- **L2 Payments:** fresh NPCI-linked UPI data and the October 2 merchant response above.
- **L3 Documents:** DigiLocker integrator process reported; selection remains unconfirmed.
- **L4 Commerce:** no date-matched ONDC or GeM change surfaced.
- **L5 Sectoral:** no new ABHA or AgriStack update surfaced.
- **L6 Governance:** no new CPGRAMS or eOffice service/policy change surfaced.
- **L7 Trust:** no new CERT-In alert, DPDP notification or TRAI order surfaced. DPDP appears here only through the attributed DigiLocker privacy assurance above—not as a new rule.

A near-window item is deliberately not counted as a fresh development: CBSE’s [Circular 12/2026 on digital payments in schools](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/documents/Promoting_Digital_Payment_01102026.pdf) is dated September 30 and was posted October 1, before this brief’s cutoff. The next checks are the first UPI data after October 15 and the actual DigiLocker empanelment terms, especially their limits on document access and retention.
