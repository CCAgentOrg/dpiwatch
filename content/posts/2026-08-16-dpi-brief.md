# DPI Brief — August 16, 2026

## 1. UPI Growth Slows as Cash Usage Quickens — MDR Debate Heats Up (L2 — Payments)

The Parliament-passed **Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026** has opened the door for a Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on UPI transactions — a first since the platform was launched in 2016. While the government insists consumer-facing transactions will stay free, the Bill creates legal room to levy charges on select high-value merchant payments (potentially above ₹2,000).

The timing is notable. An analysis by *The Hindu* published on August 15 reveals that **UPI value growth has been decelerating for five consecutive years** — from 105% in 2021-22 to 20.3% in 2025-26, and further to 18.7% in the April–August 2026 period. Meanwhile, **currency with the public surged 13% year-on-year** to ₹41.8 lakh crore as of July 31, 2026 — up from 4% growth in 2023-24. Cash, it appears, is staging a quiet comeback.

Former Chief Statistician Pronab Sen points out that the simultaneous expansion of both cash and digital transactions suggests **inflation may be understated** in official data (July CPI: 4.45%). The government has allocated ₹2,000 crore in Budget 2026-27 for UPI incentives, but the debate over who ultimately pays — banks, merchants, or consumers — is far from settled.

## 2. UPI Processes Record 23.66 Billion Transactions in July — PhonePe Widens Lead (L2 — Payments)

NPCI data released on August 15 shows UPI processed **23.66 billion transactions worth ₹29.88 lakh crore in July 2026**, a new monthly record. **PhonePe** continued to dominate with 10.86 billion transactions (up from 10.48 billion in June), while **Google Pay** held second place. Together, the two apps account for over 81% of total customer-initiated UPI volume.

The concentration remains a regulatory concern. NPCI's 30% market share cap deadline — extended to December 31, 2026 — looms, but with PhonePe still processing nearly half of all UPI transactions, compliance remains a distant prospect. The data underscores the structural paradox of India's payments revolution: a system designed for openness is dominated by two players.

## 3. Independence Day: Bhashini AI Translates PM's Speech in 22 Languages; 10 Million Youth AI Training Announced (L7 — Trust / Cross-Cutting)

On India's 80th Independence Day, PM Modi's Red Fort address was **translated in real-time into 22 Indian languages** using **Bhashini**, the open-source AI platform developed under MeitY by AI4Bharat (IIT Madras). This marks the first national-scale deployment of an AI translation pipeline for an Independence Day address.

The Bhashini pipeline chains Automatic Speech Recognition (IndicConformer), Neural Machine Translation, and Text-to-Speech output — a demonstration of India's growing sovereign AI capability layered on top of its digital infrastructure.

Modi also announced that the government would **train 10 million youth in AI skills** over the coming year, and declared that India should lead in 6G technology with indigenous solutions. The broader "Sapt Dhara" vision placed technology and innovation as one of seven pillars for Viksit Bharat 2047.

## 4. India's Cybercrime Crackdown Intensifies: 3,718 Apps Blocked, 15.75 Lakh SIMs Cancelled (L7 — Trust)

The Ministry of Home Affairs' cybercrime crackdown — led by the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) — has intensified significantly. Updated figures shared during Independence Day coverage show the government has:

- **Blocked 3,718 fraudulent mobile applications** (up from 827 in March 2026)
- **Cancelled 15.75 lakh SIM cards** linked to cybercrime operations
- **Blocked 5.77 lakh IMEI numbers** to disable compromised devices

The operation targets digital arrest scams, investment fraud, and fake call centre networks. The I4C has also blocked thousands of WhatsApp and Skype IDs used by scam syndicates. The scale of the crackdown reflects both the growing sophistication of cybercrime infrastructure and the government's willingness to use telecom-level blocking powers at population scale.

India recorded **29.44 lakh cybersecurity incidents in 2025**, an 85% increase from 2023, according to Lok Sabha data. CERT-In has empanelled 237 security auditing organisations and trained over 11 lakh participants through awareness programmes.

## 5. GeM Crosses ₹18.4 Lakh Crore Cumulative GMV; ONDC Connects 20 Crore Buyers (L4 — Commerce)

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) has achieved a **cumulative GMV of ₹18.4 lakh crore**, with over ₹5 lakh crore transacted in FY 2025-26 alone. MSEs accounted for 68% of orders (47.1% of GMV), with women-led enterprises receiving orders worth ₹28,000 crore (+28% YoY). State-level procurement grew 38.3%, indicating GeM's expanding footprint beyond central government.

Separately, ONDC has now connected **over 20 crore buyers** across 630+ cities with 1.16 lakh+ sellers, according to data highlighted during Independence Day coverage. The platform's growth trajectory suggests open commerce is gaining traction, though transaction volumes remain a fraction of UPI-enabled payments.
