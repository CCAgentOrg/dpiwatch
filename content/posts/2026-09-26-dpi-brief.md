# DPI Brief — September 26, 2026

**The Finance Minister draws the MDR line, and the ecosystem starts spending against it (L2 — Payments).** With nineteen days to the October 15 switch, Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman [insisted the 0.4% UPI merchant fee above ₹2,000 is "not a tax, cess or surcharge"](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/proposed-upi-mdr-not-a-tax-cess-or-surcharge-will-not-burden-consumers-fm-sitharaman-4159201) and will not burden consumers — the charge is shared among payment ecosystem participants to fund infrastructure and innovation. She reiterated that merchants collecting up to ₹1 lakh a month via UPI QR codes are fully exempt, a carve-out that [shields about 96% of all merchant transactions](https://urbanacres.in/upi-mdr-clarity-keeps-consumers-out-of-the-payment-charge). The exemptions map is now fully drawn: P2P transfers stay free, essential services (railways, telecom, fuel, insurance) attract a flat ₹5 above ₹2,000, and capital-markets payments get a concessional 0.02% capped at ₹300. Meanwhile the commercial response is accelerating: [PhonePe will hire over 20,000 frontline staff and deploy 50 lakh payment devices in twelve months, half in rural India](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-npci-ai-cyber-threats-mythos-upi-mdr), citing a fund that earmarks 5% of MDR collections for small-merchant expansion, and Pine Labs plans 10 lakh devices of its own. The uncomfortable part: at the SBI Banking & Economics Conclave, NPCI chief Dilip Asbe leaned on AI-enabled cyber threats to justify the fee, but did not say how much of the projected ₹13,000–15,000 crore annual collection will actually go to security, or who audits that spend.

**Biometrics replace the UPI PIN, one wallet at a time (L2 — Payments).** Samsung has [rolled out on-device biometric authentication for UPI payments in Samsung Wallet](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/samsung-wallet-gets-on-device-biometric-authentication-for-upi-payment-in-india-4159173) — fingerprint or face unlock in place of PIN entry, capped at ₹10,000 per transaction, following the RBI-NPCI push for stronger, friction-light authentication. Expect other Android OEMs and UPI apps to follow as the two-factor mandate reshapes the payment UX; the open question is liability allocation when biometrics fail on shared or borrowed phones.

**Aadhaar email updates go free — and 2.5 lakh people moved fast (L1 — Identity).** UIDAI has [enabled free email address updates through the Aadhaar app, with over 2.5 lakh residents availing the service within two days](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/uidai-enables-free-aadhaar-email-updates-via-aadhaar-app-over-2-5-lakh-users-avail-service-in-two-days). The waiver runs until [December 31, 2026](https://www.ndtv.com/offbeat/baal-aadhaar-when-is-biometric-update-necessary-12090590), and the app-based flow — no enrolment-centre visit — is part of UIDAI's broader shift of update services onto the new Aadhaar app as mAadhaar heads for retirement. A current email on file matters more than it sounds: it carries OTPs for eKYC, DigiLocker pulls, and authentication alerts. Also in the identity window: mandatory biometric updates for children at ages 5 and 15 remain [free only until September 30](https://www.ndtv.com/offbeat/baal-aadhaar-when-is-biometric-update-necessary-12090590) — after that, a ₹125 fee applies outside the first-free-update rule.

**Aarogya Manthan puts ABDM's numbers on the table — and a claims-adjudication framework (L5 — Sectoral).** Inaugurating [Aarogya Manthan 2026 marking eight years of PM-JAY](https://thenewsmill.com/2026/09/jp-nadda-inaugurates-aarogya-manthan-2026-marks-eight-years-of-pm-jay), the Union Health Minister reported the ABDM ecosystem at [more than 97.8 crore ABHAs, 121 crore linked health records, 5.6 lakh registered facilities and 11 lakh professionals](https://www.digitalhealthnews.com/union-health-minister-unveils-new-digital-health-initiatives) — and unveiled a Framework for Auto-Adjudication of Claims under AB PM-JAY, a technology-led push to cut claim settlement times. Speakers pressed for ABHA to become the default health-insurance identity, with standard treatment codes and billing formats riding the [National Health Claims Exchange](https://thenewsmill.com/2026/09/jp-nadda-inaugurates-aarogya-manthan-2026-marks-eight-years-of-pm-jay). India also showcased this stack at UNGA81 — [900 million health IDs and nearly 497 million eSanjeevani teleconsultations](https://www.digitalhealthnews.com/india-highlights-ayushman-bharat-digital-health-ecosystem-at-unga81) — while Chandigarh was [named the best-performing UT for health-record linkage](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/chandigarh/chandigarh-named-best-performing-ut-for-digital-health-records-linkage/). Auto-adjudication is the item to watch: algorithmic claim decisions at national scale will need audit trails and appeal routes, not just speed.

**West Bengal makes GeM procurement mandatory (L4 — Commerce).** The state's finance department has [made the Government e-Marketplace compulsory for procurement of goods and services available on the platform](https://newsonair.gov.in/west-bengal-govt-makes-gem-mandatory-for-procurement-of-goods-and-services), with a [three-month deadline for departments to onboard](https://newsonair.gov.in/west-bengal-govt-makes-gem-mandatory-for-procurement-of-goods-and-services). GeM, which completed ten years this August with [₹20 lakh crore in cumulative purchases — MSEs have won over ₹9 lakh crore of it, 45.6% of the total](https://yourstory.com/2026/09/tender247-brings-anupam-kher-board-expand-access-indias-rs-70-lakh-cr-tender-market) — now gains one of India's largest state buyer bases. The consumer angle: mandated marketplace buying only delivers value if listing prices are genuinely competitive, so watch whether West Bengal publishes price-comparison savings the way GeM's own studies claim.

**The DPDP compliance market opens for business (L7 — Trust).** Protean eGov Technologies [launched an Enterprise DPDP Governance & Consent Platform](https://businessupturn.com/brand-post/protean-launches-enterprise-dpdp-governance-consent-platform-at-global-fintech-fest-2) — consent management, breach-reporting workflows and data-principal rights tooling for large enterprises. The commercial timing is deliberate: [Consent Manager rules come into force November 13, 2026, and the Act's penalty-backed obligations — consent, breach notification within 72 hours, data-principal rights — go fully operational May 13, 2027](https://securityboulevard.com/2026/09/did-you-know-dpdp-is-fully-operational-from-may-13-2027). A domestic consent-infrastructure market forming around a statutory deadline is exactly how the trust layer converts from paper to plumbing; the test will be interoperability and whether consent artefacts remain citizen-portable rather than vendor-locked.

**Week ahead:** child-biometric free-update window ends — September 30; LPG Aadhaar biometric gate and trader "No UPI Day" — October 1–2; UPI MDR switch — October 15; DPDP Consent Manager rules — November 13.
